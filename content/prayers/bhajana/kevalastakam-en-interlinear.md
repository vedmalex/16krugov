### Verse 1

<center>
madhuraṁ madhurebhyo ’pi maṅgalebhyo ’pi maṅgalam
<br/>
pāvanaṁ pāvanebhyo ’pi harer nāmaiva kevalam [1]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Sweeter than all sweet things <sub>madhuraṁ madhurebhyo ’pi</sub>, more auspicious than all auspicious things <sub>maṅgalebhyo ’pi maṅgalam</sub>, purifying beyond all purifying agents <sub>pāvanaṁ pāvanebhyo ’pi</sub>—the holy name of Lord Hari alone <sub>harer nāmaiva</sub> is everything and supreme <sub>kevalam</sub>!

**Translation:**  
More sweet than all sweet things, more auspicious than all auspicious things, the supreme purifier of all purifying agents—the holy name of Lord Hari alone is everything!

### Verse 2

<center>
ābrahma-stamba-paryantaṁ sarvaṁ māyā-mayaṁ jagat
<br/>
satyaṁ satyaṁ punaḥ satyaṁ harer nāmaiva kevalam [2]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
From Lord Brahmā down to a blade of grass <sub>ābrahma-stamba-paryantaṁ</sub>, the entire <sub>sarvaṁ</sub> universe <sub>jagat</sub> is composed of illusion <sub>māyā-mayaṁ</sub>. Truth <sub>satyaṁ</sub>, truth <sub>satyaṁ</sub>, and again truth <sub>punaḥ satyaṁ</sub>: the holy name of Lord Hari alone <sub>harer nāmaiva</sub> is eternal reality <sub>kevalam</sub>!

**Translation:**  
The entire universe, from cosmic Brahmā down to a clump of grass, is a temporary manifestation of illusory energy. Truth, truth, and again truth: the holy name of Lord Hari alone is the eternal reality!

### Verse 3

<center>
sa guruḥ sa pitā cāpi sā mātā bāndhavo ’pi saḥ
<br/>
śikṣayec cet sadā smartuṁ harer nāmaiva kevalam [3]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
He is a true guru <sub>sa guruḥ</sub>, he a true father <sub>sa pitā cāpi</sub>, she a true mother <sub>sā mātā</sub>, and he a true friend <sub>bāndhavo ’pi saḥ</sub>, if they instruct <sub>śikṣayec cet</sub> to constantly <sub>sadā</sub> remember <sub>smartuṁ</sub> the holy name of Lord Hari alone <sub>harer nāmaiva kevalam</sub>!

**Translation:**  
He is a true spiritual guide, he a real father, she a genuine mother, and he a true friend and kinsman, who instructs and inspires one to always remember the holy name of Lord Hari alone!

### Verse 4

<center>
niḥśvāse nāhi viśvāsaḥ kadā ruddho bhaviṣyati
<br/>
kīrtanīya mato bālyād dharer nāmaiva kevalam [4]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
In fleeting breath <sub>niḥśvāse</sub> there is no certainty <sub>nāhi viśvāsaḥ</sub> when <sub>kadā</sub> it will be halted <sub>ruddho bhaviṣyati</sub>; therefore <sub>mataḥ</sub> should be chanted <sub>kīrtanīya</sub> from very childhood <sub>bālyād</sub> the holy name of Lord Hari alone <sub>dharer nāmaiva kevalam</sub>!

**Translation:**  
There is no certainty in our breathing—no one knows when it will abruptly cease. Therefore, one should practice chanting the holy name of Lord Hari alone from very childhood!

### Verse 5

<center>
hariḥ sadā vaset tatra yatra bhāgavatā janāḥ
<br/>
gāyanti bhakti-bhāvena harer nāmaiva kevalam [5]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Lord Hari Himself <sub>hariḥ</sub> always resides <sub>sadā vaset</sub> there <sub>tatra</sub> where <sub>yatra</sub> saintly devotees <sub>bhāgavatā janāḥ</sub> sing <sub>gāyanti</sub> in the mood of devotion <sub>bhakti-bhāvena</sub> the holy name of Lord Hari alone <sub>harer nāmaiva kevalam</sub>!

**Translation:**  
Lord Hari eternally resides in that place where exalted devotees (*bhāgavatas*) sing His holy name in the mood of pure loving devotion!

### Verse 6

<center>
aho duḥkhaṁ mahā-duḥkhaṁ duḥkhād duḥkhataraṁ yataḥ
<br/>
kācārthaṁ vismṛtaṁ ratna-harer nāmaiva kevalam [6]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Oh sorrow <sub>aho duḥkhaṁ</sub>, great sorrow <sub>mahā-duḥkhaṁ</sub>, more painful than all suffering <sub>duḥkhād duḥkhataraṁ</sub> because <sub>yataḥ</sub> for the sake of cheap glass <sub>kācārthaṁ</sub> is forgotten <sub>vismṛtaṁ</sub> the jewel <sub>ratna-</sub> of the holy name of Lord Hari alone <sub>harer nāmaiva kevalam</sub>!

**Translation:**  
Aho! What sorrow, what great grief! More agonizing than any misery in this world! For the sake of cheap broken glass, people have forgotten the priceless jewel—the holy name of Lord Hari alone!

---

### Verse 7

<center>
dīyatāṁ dīyatāṁ karṇo nīyatāṁ nīyatāṁ vacaḥ
<br/>
gīyatāṁ gīyatāṁ nityaṁ harer nāmaiva kevalam [7]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Let hearing be given again and again <sub>dīyatāṁ dīyatāṁ</sub> with the ears <sub>karṇo</sub>, let speech be directed over and over <sub>nīyatāṁ nīyatāṁ</sub> with the voice <sub>vacaḥ</sub>, let it be sung perpetually <sub>gīyatāṁ gīyatāṁ nityaṁ</sub>—the holy name of Lord Hari alone <sub>harer nāmaiva kevalam</sub>!

**Translation:**  
Give your ears to it again and again! Utter it over and over with your voice! Perpetually sing and sing the holy name of Lord Hari alone!

---

### Verse 8

<center>
tṛṇī-kṛtya jagat sarvaṁ rājate sakalopari
<br/>
cid-ānanda-mayaṁ śuddhaṁ harer nāmaiva kevalam [8]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Reducing to a blade of grass <sub>tṛṇī-kṛtya</sub> the entire <sub>sarvaṁ</sub> universe <sub>jagat</sub>, it reigns supreme <sub>rājate</sub> above everything <sub>sakalopari</sub>—full of spiritual consciousness and bliss <sub>cid-ānanda-mayaṁ</sub>, supremely pure <sub>śuddhaṁ</sub>, the holy name of Lord Hari alone <sub>harer nāmaiva kevalam</sub>!

**Translation:**  
Making the entire universe appear insignificant as a blade of grass, the holy name of Lord Hari reigns supreme above all—filled with spiritual consciousness, eternal bliss, and supreme purity!
