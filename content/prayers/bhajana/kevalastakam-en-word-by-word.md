### Verse 1

```
madhuraṁ madhurebhyo ’pi maṅgalebhyo ’pi maṅgalam
pāvanaṁ pāvanebhyo ’pi harer nāmaiva kevalam [1]
```

**Word-for-Word:**
* **madhuram** — more sweet;
* **madhurebhyaḥ api** — than all sweet things in existence;
* **maṅgalam** — more auspicious, fortunate;
* **maṅgalebhyaḥ api** — than all auspicious things;
* **pāvanam** — purifying;
* **pāvanebhyaḥ api** — than all purifying agents;
* **hareḥ nāma eva** — the holy name of Lord Hari alone;
* **kevalam** — is supreme, the sole reality and everything.

**Translation:**  
More sweet than all sweet things, more auspicious than all auspicious things, the supreme purifier of all purifying agents—the holy name of Lord Hari alone is everything!

### Verse 2

```
ābrahma-stamba-paryantaṁ sarvaṁ māyā-mayaṁ jagat
satyaṁ satyaṁ punaḥ satyaṁ harer nāmaiva kevalam [2]
```

**Word-for-Word:**
* **ābrahma-stamba-paryantam** — from creator Brahmā down to a clump of grass;
* **sarvam** — everything without exception;
* **māyā-mayam** — composed of illusory energy, temporary;
* **jagat** — the cosmic universe;
* **satyam satyam punaḥ satyam** — truth, truth, and again truth!;
* **hareḥ nāma eva kevalam** — only the holy name of Lord Hari is eternal reality.

**Translation:**  
The entire universe, from cosmic Brahmā down to a clump of grass, is a temporary manifestation of illusory energy. Truth, truth, and again truth: the holy name of Lord Hari alone is the eternal reality!

### Verse 3

```
sa guruḥ sa pitā cāpi sā mātā bāndhavo ’pi saḥ
śikṣayec cet sadā smartuṁ harer nāmaiva kevalam [3]
```

**Word-for-Word:**
* **saḥ guruḥ** — he is a genuine guru;
* **saḥ pitā ca api** — he is a real father;
* **sā mātā** — she is a true mother;
* **bāndhavaḥ api saḥ** — and he is a sincere friend and kinsman;
* **śikṣayet cet** — if they instruct and inspire;
* **sadā smartum** — to constantly remember;
* **hareḥ nāma eva kevalam** — the holy name of Lord Hari alone.

**Translation:**  
He is a true spiritual guide, he a real father, she a genuine mother, and he a true friend and kinsman, who instructs and inspires one to always remember the holy name of Lord Hari alone!

### Verse 4

```
niḥśvāse nāhi viśvāsaḥ kadā ruddho bhaviṣyati
kīrtanīya mato bālyād dharer nāmaiva kevalam [4]
```

**Word-for-Word:**
* **niḥśvāse** — in fleeting breath;
* **nāhi viśvāsaḥ** — there is no certainty, no guarantee;
* **kadā** — when;
* **ruddhaḥ bhaviṣyati** — it will suddenly be choked off;
* **ataḥ (mataḥ)** — therefore;
* **kīrtanīyam** — should be chanted;
* **bālyāt** — from very childhood;
* **hareḥ nāma eva kevalam** — the holy name of Lord Hari alone.

**Translation:**  
There is no certainty in our breathing—no one knows when it will abruptly cease. Therefore, one should practice chanting the holy name of Lord Hari alone from very childhood!

### Verse 5

```
hariḥ sadā vaset tatra yatra bhāgavatā janāḥ
gāyanti bhakti-bhāvena harer nāmaiva kevalam [5]
```

**Word-for-Word:**
* **hariḥ** — Supreme Lord Hari Himself;
* **sadā vaset** — eternally resides and dwells;
* **tatra** — in that place;
* **yatra** — where;
* **bhāgavatāḥ janāḥ** — pure, saintly devotees (*bhāgavatas*);
* **gāyanti** — sing;
* **bhakti-bhāvena** — in the ecstatic mood of pure devotion;
* **hareḥ nāma eva kevalam** — the holy name of Lord Hari alone.

**Translation:**  
Lord Hari eternally resides in that place where exalted devotees (*bhāgavatas*) sing His holy name in the mood of pure loving devotion!

### Verse 6

```
aho duḥkhaṁ mahā-duḥkhaṁ duḥkhād duḥkhataraṁ yataḥ
kācārthaṁ vismṛtaṁ ratna-harer nāmaiva kevalam [6]
```

**Word-for-Word:**
* **aho duḥkham** — oh, what sorrow!;
* **mahā-duḥkham** — what immense tragedy!;
* **duḥkhāt duḥkhataram** — more painful than all other suffering in the world;
* **yataḥ** — because;
* **kāca-artham** — for the sake of cheap shards of glass;
* **vismṛtam** — has been forgotten;
* **ratna-hareḥ nāma eva kevalam** — the wish-fulfilling jewel of the holy name of Lord Hari.

**Translation:**  
Aho! What sorrow, what great grief! More agonizing than any misery in this world! For the sake of cheap broken glass, people have forgotten the priceless jewel—the holy name of Lord Hari alone!

### Verse 7

```
dīyatāṁ dīyatāṁ karṇo nīyatāṁ nīyatāṁ vacaḥ
gīyatāṁ gīyatāṁ nityaṁ harer nāmaiva kevalam [7]
```

**Word-for-Word:**
* **dīyatām dīyatām** — let it be given again and again!;
* **karṇaḥ** — one's hearing, ears;
* **nīyatām nīyatām** — let it be engaged and directed!;
* **vacaḥ** — one's voice, faculty of speech;
* **gīyatām gīyatām** — let it be sung repeatedly!;
* **nityam** — perpetually, day after day;
* **hareḥ nāma eva kevalam** — the holy name of Lord Hari alone.

**Translation:**  
Give your ears to it again and again! Utter it over and over with your voice! Perpetually sing and sing the holy name of Lord Hari alone!

### Verse 8

```
tṛṇī-kṛtya jagat sarvaṁ rājate sakalopari
cid-ānanda-mayaṁ śuddhaṁ harer nāmaiva kevalam [8]
```

**Word-for-Word:**
* **tṛṇī-kṛtya** — making insignificant like a blade of grass;
* **jagat sarvam** — the entire universe and all its attractions;
* **rājate** — shines resplendently, reigns supreme;
* **sakala-upari** — above everything;
* **cit-ānanda-mayam** — full of spiritual consciousness and eternal bliss;
* **śuddham** — absolutely pure;
* **hareḥ nāma eva kevalam** — the holy name of Lord Hari alone.

**Translation:**  
Making the entire universe appear insignificant as a blade of grass, the holy name of Lord Hari reigns supreme above all—filled with spiritual consciousness, eternal bliss, and supreme purity!
