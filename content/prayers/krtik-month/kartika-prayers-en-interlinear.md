## 1. Śrī Gaura-āratī
**Author:** Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura (*Gītāvalī, Ārati-kīrtana, Song 2*)

---

### Verse 1

<center>
(kibā) jaya jaya gorācānder ārotiko śobhā
<br/>
jāhnavī-taṭa-vane jaga-mana-lobhā
<br/>
(gaurāṅger ārotik śobhā jaga-jana-mana-lobhā) [1]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Oh <sub>kibā</sub>, all glories <sub>jaya jaya</sub>, of Lord Gorācānd <sub>gorācānder</sub>, of the *āratī* ceremony <sub>ārotiko</sub>, to the enchanting splendor <sub>śobhā</sub>, in the grove along the bank of the Ganges <sub>jāhnavī-taṭa-vane</sub>, attracting the minds of all souls throughout the universe <sub>jaga-mana-lobhā</sub>, of Lord Gaurāṅga <sub>gaurāṅger</sub>, captivating the hearts of all living beings <sub>jaga-jana-mana-lobhā</sub>.

**Translation:**  
All glories, all glories to the beautiful *āratī* ceremony of Lord Caitanya! This Gaura-*āratī* is taking place in a grove on the banks of the Jāhnavī (Ganges) and is attracting the minds of all living entities in the universe.

---

### Verse 2

<center>
dakhiṇe nitāicānd, bāme gadādhara
<br/>
nikaṭe advaita, śrīnivāsa chatra-dhara [2]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
On His right side <sub>dakhiṇe</sub>, Lord Nitāicānd <sub>nitāicānd</sub>, on His left side <sub>bāme</sub>, Śrī Gadādhara <sub>gadādhara</sub>, nearby <sub>nikaṭe</sub>, Śrī Advaita <sub>advaita</sub>, Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura <sub>śrīnivāsa</sub>, holding an umbrella over Lord Caitanya's head <sub>chatra-dhara</sub>.

**Translation:**  
On Lord Caitanya’s right side is Lord Nityānanda, and on His left is Śrī Gadādhara. Nearby stands Śrī Advaita, and Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura is holding an umbrella over Lord Caitanya’s head.

---

### Verse 3

<center>
bosiyāche gorācānd ratna-siṁhāsane
<br/>
ārati koren brahmā-ādi deva-gaṇe [3]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Is seated <sub>bosiyāche</sub>, Lord Gorācānd <sub>gorācānd</sub>, upon a jeweled throne <sub>ratna-siṁhāsane</sub>, perform the *āratī* worship <sub>ārati koren</sub>, headed by Lord Brahmā <sub>brahmā-ādi</sub>, the demigods <sub>deva-gaṇe</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Lord Caitanya has sat down on a jeweled throne, and the demigods, headed by Lord Brahmā, perform the *āratī* ceremony.

---

### Verse 4

<center>
narahari-ādi kori’ cāmara dhulāya
<br/>
sañjaya-mukunda-bāsu-ghoṣ-ādi gāya [4]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Narahari Sarakāra and other devotees <sub>narahari-ādi</sub>, taking in hand <sub>kori’</sub>, fan Him with yak-tail whisks <sub>cāmara dhulāya</sub>, Sañjaya Paṇḍita <sub>sañjaya</sub>, Mukunda Datta <sub>mukunda</sub>, Vāsu Ghoṣa and other singers <sub>bāsu-ghoṣ-ādi</sub>, sing sweet *kīrtana* <sub>gāya</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Narahari Sarakāra and other associates of Lord Caitanya fan Him with *cāmaras*, and devotees headed by Sañjaya Paṇḍita, Mukunda Datta, and Vāsu Ghoṣa sing sweet *kīrtana*.

---

### Verse 5

<center>
śaṅkha bāje ghaṇṭā bāje bāje karatāla
<br/>
madhura mṛdaṅga bāje parama rasāla
<br/>
(śaṅkha bāje ghaṇṭā bāje madhur madhur madhur bāje) [5]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Conchshells resound <sub>śaṅkha bāje</sub>, bells ring <sub>ghaṇṭā bāje</sub>, karatālas rhythmically beat <sub>bāje karatāla</sub>, sweetly <sub>madhura</sub>, mṛdaṅga drums play <sub>mṛdaṅga bāje</sub>, supremely pleasing and relishable <sub>parama rasāla</sub>, sound wonderfully sweet <sub>madhur madhur madhur bāje</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Conchshells, bells, and *karatālas* resound, and the *mṛdaṅgas* play very sweetly. This *kīrtana* music is supremely sweet and relishable to hear.

---

### Verse 6

<center>
bahu-koṭi candra jini’ vadana ujjvala
<br/>
gala-deśe bana-mālā kore jhalamala [6]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Millions and millions <sub>bahu-koṭi</sub>, moons <sub>candra</sub>, conquering and surpassing <sub>jini’</sub>, His radiant face <sub>vadana</sub>, shines brilliantly <sub>ujjvala</sub>, around His neck <sub>gala-deśe</sub>, garland of forest flowers <sub>bana-mālā</sub>, glitters and sparkles <sub>kore jhalamala</sub>.

**Translation:**  
The brilliance of Lord Caitanya’s face conquers millions upon millions of moons, and the garland of forest flowers around His neck shines resplendently.

---

### Verse 7

<center>
śiva-śuka-nārada preme gada-gada
<br/>
bhakativinoda dekhe gorāra sampada [7]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Lord Śiva <sub>śiva</sub>, Śukadeva Gosvāmī <sub>śuka</sub>, Nārada Muni <sub>nārada</sub>, in transcendental love <sub>preme</sub>, voices trembling in ecstasy <sub>gada-gada</sub>, Ṭhākura Bhaktivinoda <sub>bhakativinoda</sub>, gazes upon <sub>dekhe</sub>, the sublime opulence and glory of Lord Gaura <sub>gorāra sampada</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Lord Śiva, Śukadeva Gosvāmī, and Nārada Muni are all present, their voices choked with the ecstasy of transcendental love. Thus Bhaktivinoda envisions the glorious opulence of Lord Śrī Gaurasundara.

## 2. Śrī Śrī Prabhupāda-padma Stavakaḥ (Prabhupāda Daśakam)
**Author:** Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Deva Gosvāmī Mahārāja  
**Meter:** Toṭaka (*Toṭaka-chhanda* — 12 syllables, 4 anapests $\smile\smile—$ per line)  
**Poetic Ornament:** *Śṛṅkhalā-bandha / Mālā-dīpaka* (the ending syllables of each line are echoed at the beginning of the next line).

---

### Verse 1

<center>
sujanārvuda-rādhita-pada-yugaṁ
<br/>
yuga-dharma-dhurandhara-pātra-varam
<br/>
varadābhaya-dāyaka-pūjya-padaṁ
<br/>
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [1]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Pious <sub>su-jana</sub>, millions <sub>arvuda</sub>, worshipped <sub>rādhita</sub>, pair of lotus feet <sub>pada-yugam</sub>, yuga-dharma <sub>yuga-dharma</sub>, the foremost bearer <sub>dhurandhara</sub>, most exalted worthy vessel <sub>pātra-varam</sub>, granter of boons <sub>varada</sub>, bestower of fearlessness <sub>abhaya-dāyaka</sub>, whose feet are ever worshipful <sub>pūjya-padam</sub>, I bow down <sub>praṇamāmi</sub>, eternally <sub>sadā</sub>, at the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda. <sub>prabhupāda-padam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
I eternally offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda, which are lovingly worshipped by millions of pure and virtuous souls. He is the pre-eminent champion carrying the standard of the religion of this age (Śrī Kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana), fulfilling all divine desires and bestowing total fearlessness upon the surrendered.

---

### Verse 2

<center>
bhajanorjjita-sajjana-saṅgha-patiṁ
<br/>
patitādhika-kāruṇikaika-gatim
<br/>
gati-vañcita-vañcakācintya-padaṁ
<br/>
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [2]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Enriched by devotional service <sub>bhajana-ūrjjita</sub>, master of the assembly of saintly devotees <sub>sat-jana-saṅgha-patim</sub>, towards the most fallen souls <sub>patita-adhika</sub>, merciful <sub>kāruṇika</sub>, sole refuge and destination <sub>eka-gatim</sub>, misleading others <sub>gati-vañcita</sub>, cheats and deceivers <sub>vañcaka</sub>, whose lotus feet are inconceivable <sub>acintya-padam</sub>, I eternally bow to the feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda. <sub>praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
He is the revered leader of the exalted assembly of saintly souls flourishing in divine loving devotion; he is the supreme ocean of kindness and the only shelter for the fallen. To cheats and deceitful sophists his lotus feet remain inconceivable, yet he reveals the genuine spiritual path to the sincere. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 3

<center>
atikomala-kāñcana-dīrgha-tanuṁ
<br/>
tanu-nindita-hema-mṛṇāla-madam
<br/>
madanārvuda-vandita-candra-padaṁ
<br/>
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [3]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Supremely soft and graceful <sub>ati-komala</sub>, golden <sub>kāñcana</sub>, tall <sub>dīrgha-tanum</sub>, whose body <sub>tanu</sub>, defeating <sub>nindita</sub>, pride of golden lotus stalks <sub>hema-mṛṇāla-madam</sub>, hundreds of millions of Cupids <sub>madana-arvuda</sub>, worshipped <sub>vandita</sub>, whose feet gleam like cooling moons <sub>candra-padam</sub>, I eternally bow to the feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda. <sub>praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
His tall, exquisitely delicate form shines with the effulgence of molten gold, completely overshadowing the beauty of golden lotus stems. The moon-like radiance of the toenails of his divine lotus feet is revered by millions of Cupids. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 4

<center>
nija-sevaka-tāraka-rañji-vidhuṁ
<br/>
vidhutāhita-huṅkṛta-siṁha-varam
<br/>
varaṇāgata-bāliśa-ṣaṇḍa-padaṁ
<br/>
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [4]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
His own dedicated servants <sub>nija-sevaka</sub>, stars <sub>tāraka</sub>, delighting <sub>rañji</sub>, like the full moon <sub>vidhum</sub>, driving away <sub>vidhuta</sub>, ill-wishers <sub>ahita</sub>, by resonant roaring <sub>huṅkṛta</sub>, best of lions <sub>siṁha-varam</sub>, who come for shelter <sub>varaṇa-āgata</sub>, simple and innocent souls <sub>bāliśa-ṣaṇḍa</sub>, supreme shelter <sub>padam</sub>, I eternally bow to the feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda. <sub>praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Like the cooling moon surrounded by glittering stars, he brings transcendental delight to the hearts of his disciples. Like the king of lions, his roaring voice of absolute truth scatters all envious adversaries, while simple and innocent souls find complete safety and auspiciousness at his feet. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 5

<center>
vipulīkṛta-vaibhava-gaura-bhuvaṁ
<br/>
bhuvaneṣu vikīrtita-gaura-dayam
<br/>
dayanīya-gaṇārpita-gaura-padaṁ
<br/>
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [5]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Greatly expanded and revealed <sub>vipulī-kṛta</sub>, opulences and glories <sub>vaibhava</sub>, the realm of Lord Gaura <sub>gaura-bhuvam</sub>, throughout all the worlds <sub>bhuvaneṣu</sub>, proclaimed <sub>vikīrtita</sub>, the causeless mercy of Lord Gaura <sub>gaura-dayam</sub>, to souls in need of mercy <sub>dayanīya-gaṇa</sub>, offered <sub>arpita</sub>, the lotus feet of Śrī Gaura <sub>gaura-padam</sub>, I eternally bow to the feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda. <sub>praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
He magnificently revealed the transcendental opulence of Śrī Gaura-dhāma; he broadcast the boundless compassion of Lord Gaurāṅga throughout all the planetary systems and installed the sacred lotus feet of Śrī Gaura within the hearts of all candidates for mercy. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 6

<center>
cira-gaura-janāśraya-viśva-guruṁ
<br/>
guru-gaura-kiśoraka-dāsya-param
<br/>
paramādṛta-bhaktivinoda-padaṁ
<br/>
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [6]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Eternal <sub>cira</sub>, shelter of the servants of Gaura <sub>gaura-jana-āśraya</sub>, Jagad-guru <sub>viśva-gurum</sub>, unto his spiritual master <sub>guru</sub>, Śrīla Gaura Kiśora dāsa Bābājī <sub>gaura-kiśoraka</sub>, exclusively dedicated to service <sub>dāsya-param</sub>, deeply venerating <sub>parama-ādṛta</sub>, the lotus feet of Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura <sub>bhaktivinoda-padam</sub>, I eternally bow to the feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda. <sub>praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
He is the everlasting sanctuary of the devotees of Gaura and the preceptor of the entire universe. He is wholeheartedly surrendered in unalloyed servitude to his spiritual master, Śrīla Gaura Kiśora dāsa Bābājī Mahārāja, and he reveres the divine feet of Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura above all else. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 7

<center>
raghu-rūpa-sanātana-kīrti-dharaṁ
<br/>
dharaṇī-tala-kīrtita-jīva-kavim
<br/>
kavirāja-narottama-sakhya-padaṁ
<br/>
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [7]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Of Raghunātha <sub>raghu-rūpa-sanātana</sub>, bearer of the spotless glory <sub>kīrti-dharam</sub>, throughout the earth <sub>dharaṇī-tala</sub>, celebrated <sub>kīrtita</sub>, as the seer-scholar non-different from Śrī Jīva <sub>jīva-kavim</sub>, with Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja and Narottama dāsa Ṭhākura <sub>kavirāja-narottama</sub>, sharing divine intimate companionship <sub>sakhya-padam</sub>, I eternally bow to the feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda. <sub>praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
He proudly upholds the banner of transcendental renown of Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, and Raghunātha. Throughout the surface of the earth, he is glorified as the embodiment of the profound philosophical vision of Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī, and he enjoys intimate camaraderie with Śrīla Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja and Ṭhākura Narottama. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 8

<center>
kṛpayā hari-kīrtana-mūrti-dharaṁ
<br/>
dharaṇī-bhara-hāraka-gaura-janam
<br/>
janakādhika-vatsala-snigdha-padaṁ
<br/>

</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Out of causeless compassion <sub>kṛpayā</sub>, manifest as the personified embodiment of Hari-kīrtana <sub>hari-kīrtana-mūrti-dharam</sub>, lifting the burden from mother earth <sub>dharaṇī-bhara-hāraka</sub>, confidential eternal associate of Śrī Gaura <sub>gaura-janam</sub>, exceeding that of a father <sub>janaka-adhika</sub>, affectionate <sub>vatsala</sub>, fountain of soothing tenderness <sub>snigdha-padam</sub>, I eternally bow to the feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda. <sub>praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Out of supreme compassion he has manifested as the very embodiment of congregational Hari-kīrtana. An intimate companion of Lord Gaura, he alleviates the burdensome sins of Mother Earth; his gentle lotus feet radiate a compassionate parental affection far surpassing that of an earthly father. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 9

<center>

<br/>

To surrendered servants <sub>śaraṇāgata-kiṅkara</sub>, wish-fulfilling tree <sub>kalpa-tarum</sub>, putting ordinary trees to shame <sub>taru-dhik-kṛta</sub>, patient <sub>dhīra</sub>, crown of magnanimous givers <sub>vadānya-varam</sub>, by the foremost bestowers of benedictions <sub>varada-indra-gaṇa</sub>, worshipped <sub>arcita</sub>, divine lotus feet <sub>divya-padam</sub>, I eternally bow to the feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda. <sub>praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam</sub>.

He is a transcendental desire-tree fulfilling every aspiration of his surrendered servants. His profound forbearance and supreme magnanimity put even the trees to shame. His divine lotus feet are revered even by the foremost rulers and benefactors of the cosmos. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 10

<center>
paramahaṁsa-varaṁ paramārtha-patiṁ
<br/>
patitoddharaṇe kṛta-veṣa-yatim

Best among the paramahaṁsas <sub>paramahaṁsa-varam</sub>, master of the supreme goal <sub>paramārtha-patim</sub>, for delivering the fallen souls <sub>patita-uddharaṇe</sub>, who assumed the garb of a renunciant ascetic <sub>kṛta-veṣa-yatim</sub>, by the assemblies of kingly renunciants <sub>yati-rāja-gaṇaiḥ</sub>, whose feet are served with utter reverence <sub>parisevya-padam</sub>, I eternally bow to the feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda. <sub>praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam</sub>.

He is the crown jewel of paramahaṁsas, the master of the fifth and supreme pursuit of life (Śrī Kṛṣṇa-prema). Solely to deliver fallen souls, he accepted the austere saffron dress of a renunciant ascetic. His lotus feet are devoutly served by the emperors among sannyāsīs. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 11

<center>

<br/>

**Translation:**  
He is the dearmost, intimate maidservant of the Beloved of King Vṛṣabhānu's daughter (Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī). Taking immense refuge, I place the dust of his holy feet upon my head, and I eternally offer my pranāmas unto the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda, which are imbued with Her wondrous, all-purifying potency!

## 3. Śrī Śrī Ṣaḍ-Gosvāmy-aṣṭakam
**Author:** Śrīla Śrīnivāsa Ācārya  
**Meter:** Śārdūlavikrīḍita (19 syllables per line)  
**Refrain:** *vande rūpa-sanātanau raghu-yugau śrī-jīva-gopālakau*  
*(“I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Six Gosvāmīs — Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, the two Raghunāthas [Bhaṭṭa and Dāsa], Śrī Jīva, and Gopāla Bhaṭṭa.”)*

---

### Verse 1

<center>
kṛṣṇotkīrtana-gāna-nartana-parau premāmṛtāmbho-nidhī
<br/>
dhīrādhīra-jana-priyau priya-karau nirmatsarau pūjitau
<br/>

The supreme romantic hero of Vṛndāvana <sub>vṛndāvana-su-nāgaram</sub>, attired in garments suited for amorous sports <sub>vilāsa-anuga-vāsasam</sub>, who shattered the false pride of King Indra <sub>sura-indra-garva-mocanam</sub>, I bow down unto the son of Nanda. <sub>namāmi nanda-nandanam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the son of Nanda, the consummate hero of Vṛndāvana attired in dress perfect for playful pastimes, who humbled and pulverized the arrogant pride of Indra.

### Verse 8

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the son of Nanda, the dearmost hero of the gopīs of Vraja, who constantly showers them with transcendental ecstasy and whose incomparable beauty enchants every mind in creation.

## 7. Śrī Dāmodarāṣṭakam
**Source:** *Padma Purāṇa* (Spoken by Satyavrata Muni)  
**The Primary Hymn of Kārtika Month**

---

### Verse 1

<center>
namāmīśvaraṁ sac-cid-ānanda-rūpaṁ
<br/>
lasat-kuṇḍalaṁ gokule bhrājamānam
<br/>
yaśodā-bhiyolūkhalād dhāvamānaṁ
<br/>
parāmṛṣṭam atyantato drutya gopyā [1]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
I bow down <sub>namāmi</sub>, unto the Supreme Controller <sub>īśvaram</sub>, whose form is eternal <sub>sac-cid-ānanda-rūpam</sub>, whose earrings sway and glisten <sub>lasat-kuṇḍalam</sub>, shining splendidly in Gokula <sub>gokule bhrājamānam</sub>, out of fear of Mother Yaśodā <sub>yaśodā-bhiyā</sub>, from the wooden grinding mortar <sub>ulūkhalāt</sub>, running away <sub>dhāvamānam</sub>, caught from behind <sub>parāmṛṣṭam</sub>, running very fast <sub>atyantataḥ drutya</sub>, by the cowherd lady . <sub>gopyā</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Unto the Supreme Lord, whose form is the embodiment of eternity, knowledge, and bliss, whose shark-shaped earrings playfully swing and shine, and who radiates splendidly in Gokula; who, in fear of Mother Yaśodā, fled from the grinding mortar but was caught from behind as she ran swiftly after Him — unto that Supreme Lord, I offer my humble obeisances.

---

### Verse 2

<center>
rudantaṁ muhur netra-yugmaṁ mṛjantaṁ
<br/>
karāmbhoja-yugmena sātaṅka-netram
<br/>
muhuḥ śvāsa-kampa-tri-rekhāṅka-kaṇṭha-
<br/>
sthita-graiva-dāmodaraṁ bhakti-baddham [2]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Crying <sub>rudantam</sub>, again and again <sub>muhuḥ</sub>, both His eyes <sub>netra-yugmam</sub>, rubbing <sub>mṛjantam</sub>, with both His lotus hands <sub>kara-ambhoja-yugmena</sub>, with eyes full of fear <sub>sa-ātaṅka-netram</sub>, whose breath heaves <sub>muhuḥ śvāsa-kampa</sub>, upon His neck marked with three lines <sub>tri-rekhā-aṅka-kaṇṭha</sub>, pearl necklace <sub>sthita-graiva</sub>, Lord Dāmodara <sub>dāmodaram</sub>, bound solely by devotion. <sub>bhakti-baddham</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Crying repeatedly and rubbing both His frightened eyes with His two lotus hands, His neck—adorned with three lines like a conch shell—quivers from quick breathing, shaking the pearl necklace upon His chest. I bow to that Lord Dāmodara, whose belly is bound not by mere ropes, but by the pure love of His devotee.

---

### Verse 3

<center>
itīdṛk sva-līlābhir ānanda-kuṇḍe
<br/>
sva-ghoṣaṁ nimajjantam ākhyāpayantam
<br/>
tadīyeśita-jñeṣu bhaktair jitatvaṁ
<br/>
punaḥ prematas taṁ śatāvṛtti vande [3]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
By such <sub>iti-īdṛk</sub>, pastimes <sub>sva-līlābhiḥ</sub>, into lakes of ecstatic bliss <sub>ānanda-kuṇḍe</sub>, all the residents of Gokula <sub>sva-ghoṣam</sub>, immersing <sub>nimajjantam</sub>, revealing <sub>ākhyāpayantam</sub>, to those who know His sovereign majesty <sub>tadīya-īśita-jñeṣu</sub>, that He is conquered only by pure love <sub>bhaktaiḥ jitatvam</sub>, again with deep love <sub>punaḥ premataḥ</sub>, unto Him <sub>tam</sub>, hundreds of times <sub>śata-āvṛtti</sub>, I offer my obeisances. <sub>vande</sub>.

**Translation:**  
By such childhood pastimes He plunges the cowherd settlement of Gokula into pools of transcendental ecstasy, demonstrating to those absorbed in knowledge of His majesty that He is conquered only by pure intimate love. Hundreds and hundreds of times, with deepest affection, I offer my praises unto that Lord Dāmodara.

---

### Verse 4

<center>
varaṁ deva mokṣaṁ na mokṣāvadhiṁ vā
<br/>
na cānyaṁ vṛṇe 'haṁ vareśād apīha
<br/>
idaṁ te vapur nātha gopāla-bālaṁ
<br/>
sadā me manasy āvirāstāṁ kim anyaiḥ [4]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Boons <sub>varam</sub>, O Lord! <sub>deva</sub>, impersonal liberation <sub>mokṣam</sub>, not <sub>na</sub>, nor the highest limit of liberation <sub>mokṣa-avadhim vā</sub>, nor any other benediction <sub>na ca anyam</sub>, do I pray for <sub>vṛṇe aham</sub>, even from You <sub>vara-īśāt api iha</sub>, this divine form of Yours <sub>idam te vapuḥ</sub>, O master! <sub>nātha</sub>, young cowherd boy <sub>gopāla-bālam</sub>, eternally <sub>sadā</sub>, within my mind <sub>me manasi</sub>, may it ever manifest <sub>āvirāstām</sub>, of what use are any other boons? <sub>kim anyaiḥ</sub>.

**Translation:**  
O Lord, although You are able to grant every boon, I do not seek impersonal liberation, nor the highest summit of salvation in Vaikuṇṭha, nor any other reward. My only longing, O Lord, is that this form of Yours as the child cowherd boy of Gokula may ever remain manifest within my heart. What use have I for any other gift?

---

### Verse 5

<center>
idaṁ te mukhāmbhojam atyanta-nīlair
<br/>
vṛtaṁ kuntalaiḥ snigdha-raktaiś ca gopyā
<br/>
muhuś cumbitaṁ bimba-raktādharaṁ me
<br/>
manasy āvirāstām alaṁ lakṣa-lābhaiḥ [5]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
This lotus face of Yours <sub>idam te mukha-ambhojam</sub>, by deep black curls <sub>atyanta-nīlaiḥ</sub>, encircled by locks of hair <sub>vṛtam kuntalaiḥ</sub>, glossy with a reddish tinge <sub>snigdha-raktaiḥ</sub>, by mother Yaśodā <sub>ca gopyā</sub>, kissed again and again <sub>muhuḥ cumbitam</sub>, with lips red like ripe bimba fruit <sub>bimba-rakta-adharam</sub>, may it manifest in my mind <sub>me manasi āvirāstām</sub>, what need is there? <sub>alam</sub>, of hundreds of thousands of other gains? <sub>lakṣa-lābhaiḥ</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Your lotus face is framed by soft, dark curling locks tinged with red, and Mother Yaśodā showers kisses upon Your lips, which are as crimson as the bimba fruit. May this vision of Your lotus face always remain clearly manifest within my heart. Of what value are hundreds of thousands of other achievements to me?

---

### Verse 6

<center>
namo deva dāmodarānanta viṣṇo
<br/>
prasīda prabho duḥkha-jālābdhi-magnam
<br/>
kṛpā-dṛṣṭi-vṛṣṭyāti-dīnaṁ batānu-
<br/>
gṛhāṇeśa mām ajñam edhy akṣi-dṛśyaḥ [6]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Obeisances <sub>namaḥ deva</sub>, O Dāmodara! <sub>dāmodara</sub>, O Infinite One! <sub>ananta</sub>, O all-pervading Viṣṇu! <sub>viṣṇo</sub>, be pleased with me <sub>prasīda prabho</sub>, drowning in the ocean of miseries <sub>duḥkha-jāla-abdhi-magnam</sub>, by the downpour of Your glance of mercy <sub>kṛpā-dṛṣṭi-vṛṣṭyā</sub>, most fallen and destitute <sub>ati-dīnam</sub>, alas <sub>bata</sub>, please accept and shower favor <sub>anugṛhāṇa</sub>, O Supreme Lord! <sub>īśa</sub>, upon me <sub>mām ajñam</sub>, please appear before my eyes! <sub>edhi akṣi-dṛśyaḥ</sub>.

**Translation:**  
O Supreme Lord! O Dāmodara! O Infinite One! O Viṣṇu! Be pleased with me, my Master! I am sinking helplessly in an ocean of worldly suffering. Shower upon me the rain of Your merciful glance, deliver this ignorant soul, and become visible before my eyes!

---

### Verse 7

<center>
kuberātmajau baddha-mūrtyaiva yadvat
<br/>
tvayā mocitau bhakti-bhājau kṛtau ca
<br/>

<br/>
na mokṣe graho me 'sti dāmodareha [7]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
The two sons of Kuvera <sub>kubera-ātmajau</sub>, by Your bound form <sub>baddha-mūrtyā eva</sub>, just as <sub>yadvat</sub>, by You <sub>tvayā</sub>, were liberated <sub>mocitau</sub>, and awarded devotional service <sub>bhakti-bhājau kṛtau ca</sub>, in that very manner <sub>tathā</sub>, Your own prema-bhakti <sub>prema-bhaktim svakām</sub>, please bestow upon me <sub>me prayaccha</sub>, I have no longing for liberation <sub>na mokṣe grahaḥ me asti</sub>, O Lord Dāmodara! <sub>dāmodara</sub>, here. <sub>iha</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Just as You liberated the two sons of Kuvera (Nalakūvara and Maṇigrīva) from their curse as twin arjuna trees even while You were bound to the mortar, awarding them the treasure of pure devotion, please likewise bestow upon me Your own prema-bhakti! I have no desire for mere liberation, O Lord Dāmodara.

### Verse 8

Let my obeisances be <sub>namaḥ te astu</sub>, unto the rope <sub>dāmne</sub>, abode of brilliant effulgence <sub>sphurat-dīpti-dhāmne</sub>, unto Your divine belly <sub>tvadīya-udarāya</sub>, and <sub>atha</sub>, the shelter of the entire universe <sub>viśvasya dhāmne</sub>, obeisances unto Śrīmatī Rādhikā <sub>namaḥ rādhikāyai</sub>, Your most beloved <sub>tvadīya-priyāyai</sub>, who manifests limitless pastimes <sub>namaḥ ananta-līlāya</sub>, unto You <sub>devāya tubhyam</sub>.

I offer my respectful obeisances unto the effulgent rope that binds Your abdomen, and unto Your sacred belly, which is the resting place of the entire cosmos! I offer my respectful obeisances unto Śrīmatī Rādhikā, Your supreme beloved, and unto You, the Supreme Personality of Godhead, who manifest infinite divine pastimes!

## 8. Offering of Lamps (Dīpa-dāna) to Śrī Śrī Rādhikā-Keśava Rāya Jī 🪔

### Mantras and Prayer for Offering the Ghee Lamp

When offering the sacred ghee lamp (*dīpa*) during Kārtika Maas, devotees meditate upon the divine forms of Śrī Śrī Rādhikā and Keśava Rāya:

<center>
śrī-kṛṣṇa rādhikā-kānta kṛpā-sindho jagat-pate
<br/>
gopeśa gopikā-kānta rādhā-kānta namo 'stu te
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
O Śrī Kṛṣṇa <sub>śrī-kṛṣṇa</sub>, beloved of Rādhikā <sub>rādhikā-kānta</sub>, ocean of mercy <sub>kṛpā-sindho</sub>, master of the universe <sub>jagat-pate</sub>, Lord of the cowherds <sub>gopeśa</sub>, beloved of the gopīs <sub>gopikā-kānta</sub>, lover of Śrī Rādhā <sub>rādhā-kānta</sub>, let obeisances be unto You <sub>namo 'stu te</sub>!

---

### Lamp Offering Prayer

<center>
dāmodarāṣṭakaṁ kṛtvā dīpa-dānaṁ karomi te
<br/>
rādhikā-sahitaṁ vande keśavaṁ kleśa-nāśanam
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Having recited the Dāmodarāṣṭakam <sub>dāmodarāṣṭakaṁ kṛtvā</sub>, I offer this lamp unto You <sub>dīpa-dānaṁ karomi te</sub>; accompanied by Śrīmatī Rādhikā <sub>rādhikā-sahitaṁ</sub>, I offer praise <sub>vande</sub> unto Lord Keśava <sub>keśavaṁ</sub>, the destroyer of all afflictions <sub>kleśa-nāśanam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Having recited the Dāmodarāṣṭakam, I lovingly offer this sacred ghee lamp unto You, O Lord Dāmodara! I offer my respectful obeisances unto Lord Keśava Rāya accompanied by His eternal consort Śrīmatī Rādhikā, who removes all material miseries and bestows pure devotional love!
