### ISKCON Goshala (Yajña-śālā) Daily Recitation during Evening Āratī

## 1. Śrī Gaura-āratī
**Author:** Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura (*Gītāvalī, Ārati-kīrtana, Song 2*)  

---

### Verse 1

```
(kibā) jaya jaya gorācānder ārotiko śobhā
jāhnavī-taṭa-vane jaga-mana-lobhā
(gaurāṅger ārotik śobhā jaga-jana-mana-lobhā) [1]
```

**Translation:**  
All glories, all glories to the beautiful *āratī* ceremony of Lord Caitanya! This Gaura-*āratī* is taking place in a grove on the banks of the Jāhnavī (Ganges) and is attracting the minds of all living entities in the universe.

---

### Verse 2

```
dakhiṇe nitāicānd, bāme gadādhara
nikaṭe advaita, śrīnivāsa chatra-dhara [2]
```

**Translation:**  
On Lord Caitanya’s right side is Lord Nityānanda, and on His left is Śrī Gadādhara. Nearby stands Śrī Advaita, and Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura is holding an umbrella over Lord Caitanya’s head.

---

### Verse 3

```
bosiyāche gorācānd ratna-siṁhāsane
ārati koren brahmā-ādi deva-gaṇe [3]
```

**Translation:**  
Lord Caitanya has sat down on a jeweled throne, and the demigods, headed by Lord Brahmā, perform the *āratī* ceremony.

---

### Verse 4

```
narahari-ādi kori’ cāmara dhulāya
sañjaya-mukunda-bāsu-ghoṣ-ādi gāya [4]
```

**Translation:**  
Narahari Sarakāra and other associates of Lord Caitanya fan Him with *cāmaras*, and devotees headed by Sañjaya Paṇḍita, Mukunda Datta, and Vāsu Ghoṣa sing sweet *kīrtana*.

---

### Verse 5

```
śaṅkha bāje ghaṇṭā bāje bāje karatāla
madhura mṛdaṅga bāje parama rasāla
(śaṅkha bāje ghaṇṭā bāje madhur madhur madhur bāje) [5]
```

**Translation:**  
Conchshells, bells, and *karatālas* resound, and the *mṛdaṅgas* play very sweetly. This *kīrtana* music is supremely sweet and relishable to hear.

---

### Verse 6

```
bahu-koṭi candra jini’ vadana ujjvala
gala-deśe bana-mālā kore jhalamala [6]
```

**Translation:**  
The brilliance of Lord Caitanya’s face conquers millions upon millions of moons, and the garland of forest flowers around His neck shines resplendently.

---

### Verse 7

```
śiva-śuka-nārada preme gada-gada
bhakativinoda dekhe gorāra sampada [7]
```

**Translation:**  
Lord Śiva, Śukadeva Gosvāmī, and Nārada Muni are all present, their voices choked with the ecstasy of transcendental love. Thus Bhaktivinoda envisions the glorious opulence of Lord Śrī Gaurasundara.

---

## 2. Śrī Śrī Prabhupāda-padma Stavakaḥ (Prabhupāda Daśakam)
**Author:** Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Deva Gosvāmī Mahārāja  
**Meter:** Toṭaka (*Toṭaka-chhanda* — 12 syllables, 4 anapests $\smile\smile—$ per line)  
**Poetic Ornament:** *Śṛṅkhalā-bandha / Mālā-dīpaka* (the ending syllables of each line are echoed at the beginning of the next line).

---

### Verse 1

```
sujanārvuda-rādhita-pada-yugaṁ
yuga-dharma-dhurandhara-pātra-varam
varadābhaya-dāyaka-pūjya-padaṁ
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [1]
```

**Translation:**  
I eternally offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda, which are lovingly worshipped by millions of pure and virtuous souls. He is the pre-eminent champion carrying the standard of the religion of this age (Śrī Kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana), fulfilling all divine desires and bestowing total fearlessness upon the surrendered.

---

### Verse 2

```
bhajanorjjita-sajjana-saṅgha-patiṁ
patitādhika-kāruṇikaika-gatim
gati-vañcita-vañcakācintya-padaṁ
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [2]
```

**Translation:**  
He is the revered leader of the exalted assembly of saintly souls flourishing in divine loving devotion; he is the supreme ocean of kindness and the only shelter for the fallen. To cheats and deceitful sophists his lotus feet remain inconceivable, yet he reveals the genuine spiritual path to the sincere. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 3

```
atikomala-kāñcana-dīrgha-tanuṁ
tanu-nindita-hema-mṛṇāla-madam
madanārvuda-vandita-candra-padaṁ
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [3]
```

**Translation:**  
His tall, exquisitely delicate form shines with the effulgence of molten gold, completely overshadowing the beauty of golden lotus stems. The moon-like radiance of the toenails of his divine lotus feet is revered by millions of Cupids. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 4

```
nija-sevaka-tāraka-rañji-vidhuṁ
vidhutāhita-huṅkṛta-siṁha-varam
varaṇāgata-bāliśa-ṣaṇḍa-padaṁ
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [4]
```

**Translation:**  
Like the cooling moon surrounded by glittering stars, he brings transcendental delight to the hearts of his disciples. Like the king of lions, his roaring voice of absolute truth scatters all envious adversaries, while simple and innocent souls find complete safety and auspiciousness at his feet. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 5

```
vipulīkṛta-vaibhava-gaura-bhuvaṁ
bhuvaneṣu vikīrtita-gaura-dayam
dayanīya-gaṇārpita-gaura-padaṁ
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [5]
```

**Translation:**  
He magnificently revealed the transcendental opulence of Śrī Gaura-dhāma; he broadcast the boundless compassion of Lord Gaurāṅga throughout all the planetary systems and installed the sacred lotus feet of Śrī Gaura within the hearts of all candidates for mercy. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 6

```
cira-gaura-janāśraya-viśva-guruṁ
guru-gaura-kiśoraka-dāsya-param
paramādṛta-bhaktivinoda-padaṁ
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [6]
```

**Translation:**  
He is the everlasting sanctuary of the devotees of Gaura and the preceptor of the entire universe. He is wholeheartedly surrendered in unalloyed servitude to his spiritual master, Śrīla Gaura Kiśora dāsa Bābājī Mahārāja, and he reveres the divine feet of Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura above all else. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 7

```
raghu-rūpa-sanātana-kīrti-dharaṁ
dharaṇī-tala-kīrtita-jīva-kavim
kavirāja-narottama-sakhya-padaṁ
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [7]
```

**Translation:**  
He proudly upholds the banner of transcendental renown of Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, and Raghunātha. Throughout the surface of the earth, he is glorified as the embodiment of the profound philosophical vision of Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī, and he enjoys intimate camaraderie with Śrīla Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja and Ṭhākura Narottama. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 8

```
kṛpayā hari-kīrtana-mūrti-dharaṁ
dharaṇī-bhara-hāraka-gaura-janam
janakādhika-vatsala-snigdha-padaṁ
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [8]
```

**Translation:**  
Out of supreme compassion he has manifested as the very embodiment of congregational Hari-kīrtana. An intimate companion of Lord Gaura, he alleviates the burdensome sins of Mother Earth; his gentle lotus feet radiate a compassionate parental affection far surpassing that of an earthly father. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 9

```
śaraṇāgata-kiṅkara-kalpa-taruṁ
taru-dhik-kṛta-dhīra-vadānya-varam
varadendra-gaṇārcita-divya-padaṁ
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [9]
```

**Translation:**  
He is a transcendental desire-tree fulfilling every aspiration of his surrendered servants. His profound forbearance and supreme magnanimity put even the trees to shame. His divine lotus feet are revered even by the foremost rulers and benefactors of the cosmos. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 10

```
paramahaṁsa-varaṁ paramārtha-patiṁ
patitoddharaṇe kṛta-veṣa-yatim
yati-rāja-gaṇaiḥ parisevya-padaṁ
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [10]
```

**Translation:**  
He is the crown jewel of paramahaṁsas, the master of the fifth and supreme pursuit of life (Śrī Kṛṣṇa-prema). Solely to deliver fallen souls, he accepted the austere saffron dress of a renunciant ascetic. His lotus feet are devoutly served by the emperors among sannyāsīs. I eternally bow to the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda.

---

### Verse 11

```
vṛṣabhānu-sutā-dayitānucaraṁ
caraṇāśrita-reṇu-dharas tam aham
mahad-adbhuta-pāvana-śakti-padaṁ
praṇamāmi sadā prabhupāda-padam [11]
```

**Translation:**  
He is the dearmost, intimate maidservant of the Beloved of King Vṛṣabhānu's daughter (Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī). Taking immense refuge, I place the dust of his holy feet upon my head, and I eternally offer my pranāmas unto the lotus feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda, which are imbued with Her wondrous, all-purifying potency!

---

## 3. Śrī Śrī Ṣaḍ-Gosvāmy-aṣṭakam
**Author:** Śrīla Śrīnivāsa Ācārya  
**Meter:** Śārdūlavikrīḍita (19 syllables per line)  
**Refrain:** *vande rūpa-sanātanau raghu-yugau śrī-jīva-gopālakau*  
*(“I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Six Gosvāmīs — Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, the two Raghunāthas [Bhaṭṭa and Dāsa], Śrī Jīva, and Gopāla Bhaṭṭa.”)*

---

### Verse 1

```
kṛṣṇotkīrtana-gāna-nartana-parau premāmṛtāmbho-nidhī
dhīrādhīra-jana-priyau priya-karau nirmatsarau pūjitau
śrī-caitanya-kṛpā-bharau bhuvi bhuvo bhārāvahantārakau
vande rūpa-sanātanau raghu-yugau śrī-jīva-gopālakau [1]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Six Gosvāmīs — Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa, Raghunātha dāsa, Śrī Jīva, and Gopāla Bhaṭṭa — who are always engaged in chanting the holy name of Kṛṣṇa and dancing in ecstasy. They are expansive oceans of the nectar of divine love, and they are beloved by the gentle and the ruffian alike because they are completely non-envious. Showered with the fullness of Lord Caitanya's mercy, they lift the burdensome grief from the earth and deliver all conditioned souls.

---

### Verse 2

```
nānā-śāstra-vicāraṇaika-nipuṇau sad-dharma-saṁsthāpakau
lokānāṁ hita-kāriṇau tri-bhuvane mānyau śaraṇyākarau
rādhā-kṛṣṇa-padāravinda-bhajanānandena mattālikau
vande rūpa-sanātanau raghu-yugau śrī-jīva-gopālakau [2]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Six Gosvāmīs, who are supremely expert in scrutinizingly studying all the scriptures in order to firmly establish eternal religious principles for the true benefit of all people. Honored throughout the three worlds, they are the fountainhead of refuge, behaving like intoxicated bees enchanted by the bliss of worshipping the lotus feet of Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.

---

### Verse 3

```
śrī-gaurāṅga-guṇānuvarṇana-vidhau śraddhā-samṛddhy-anvitau
pāpottāpa-nikṛntanau tanu-bhṛtāṁ govinda-gānāmṛtaiḥ
ānandāmbudhi-vardhanaika-nipuṇau kaivalya-nistārakau
vande rūpa-sanātanau raghu-yugau śrī-jīva-gopālakau [3]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Six Gosvāmīs, who are rich in unswerving faith while describing the qualities of Lord Gaurāṅga. Through the nectar of their songs praising Govinda, they extinguish the scorching tribulations of all embodied beings, expanding the ocean of divine joy and rescuing fallen souls from the dark void of impersonal liberation.

---

### Verse 4

```
tyaktvā tūrṇam aśeṣa-maṇḍala-pati-śreṇīṁ sadā tucchavat
bhūtvā dīna-gaṇeśakau karuṇayā kaupīna-kanthāśritau
gopī-bhāva-rasāmṛtābdhi-laharī-kallola-magnau muhur
vande rūpa-sanātanau raghu-yugau śrī-jīva-gopālakau [4]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Six Gosvāmīs, who swiftly cast aside all aristocratic opulence and companionship of wealthy kings as trivial trash. Out of pure mercy for the destitute, they donned mere loincloths and patched garments, continuously submerging themselves in the tumultuous nectar waves of the gopīs' ecstatic moods.

---

### Verse 5

```
kūjat-kokila-haṁsa-sārasa-gaṇākīrṇe mayūrākule
nānā-ratna-nibaddha-mūla-viṭapa-śrī-yukta-vṛndāvane
rādhā-kṛṣṇam ahar-niśaṁ prabhajatau jīvārthadau yau mudā
vande rūpa-sanātanau raghu-yugau śrī-jīva-gopālakau [5]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Six Gosvāmīs, who day and night lovingly worship Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in the divine groves of Vṛndāvana, where cuckoos, swans, and cranes warble and peacocks dance, and where the roots of trees are set with transcendental gems. With supreme joy, they bestow upon all souls the ultimate treasure of existence.

---

### Verse 6

```
saṅkhyā-pūrvaka-nāma-gāna-natibhiḥ kālāvasānī-kṛtau
nidrāhāra-vihārakādi-vijitau cātyanta-dīnau ca yau
rādhā-kṛṣṇa-guṇa-smṛter madhurimānandena sammohitau
vande rūpa-sanātanau raghu-yugau śrī-jīva-gopālakau [6]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Six Gosvāmīs, who passed their entire time engaged in counted rounds of the Holy Name and prostrated obeisances. They thoroughly mastered sleep, eating, and all bodily demands; remaining exceedingly humble and meek, they were perpetually enchanted by the ecstatic bliss born of remembering the sweet qualities of Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.

---

### Verse 7

```
rādhā-kuṇḍa-taṭe kalinda-tanayā-tīre ca vaṁśīvaṭe
premonmāda-vaśād aśeṣa-daśayā grastau pramattau sadā
gāyantau ca kadā harer guṇa-varaṁ bhāvābhibhūtau mudā
vande rūpa-sanātanau raghu-yugau śrī-jīva-gopālakau [7]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Six Gosvāmīs—namely Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī, Śrī Sanātana Gosvāmī, the two Raghunāthas, Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī, and Śrī Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī—who were sometimes on the banks of Rādhā-kuṇḍa, sometimes on the shores of the Yamunā (Kalinda-tanayā), and sometimes at Vaṁśīvaṭa. There, seized by the divine madness of prema, they perpetually appeared like madmen, overwhelmed by innumerable ecstatic states, joyfully singing the supreme qualities of Lord Hari.

---

### Verse 8

```
he rādhe! vraja-devike! ca lalite! he nanda-sūno! kutaḥ
śrī-govardhana-kalpa-pādapa-tale kālindī-vanye kutaḥ
ghoṣantāv iti sarvato vraja-pure khedair mahā-vihvalau
vande rūpa-sanātanau raghu-yugau śrī-jīva-gopālakau [8]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Six Gosvāmīs, who wept and wandered through the pastures of Vraja, loudly calling: "O Rādhā! O Queen of Vraja! O dear Lalitā! O son of Nanda! Where are You all now? Are You residing at the base of the wish-fulfilling trees of Mount Govardhana, or in the solitary groves along the Yamunā? Where are You?" Agonized in divine separation, they offered their eternal hearts.

## 4. Śrī Śacī-tanayāṣṭakam
**Author:** Śrīla Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya  
**Dedicated to:** Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu, the son of Śrīmatī Śacīdevī  
**Refrain:** *taṁ praṇamāmi ca śrī-śacī-tanayam*  
*(“Unto that son of Śacīdevī I offer my humble obeisances.”)*

---

### Verse 1

```
ujjvala-varaṇa-gaura-vara-dehaṁ
vilasita-niravadhi-bhāva-videham
tri-bhuvana-pāvana-kṛpayā leśaṁ
taṁ praṇamāmi ca śrī-śacī-tanayam [1]
```

**Translation:**  
His transcendentally beautiful body shines with a radiant golden hue; He is ceaselessly overwhelmed by ecstatic spiritual moods, completely oblivious to all physical awareness. A mere fraction of His causeless mercy is sufficient to liberate the three worlds. Unto that divine son of Mother Śacī, I offer my humble obeisances.

---

### Verse 2

```
gadgada-antara-bhāva-vikāraṁ
durjana-tarjana-nāda-viśālam
bhava-bhaya-bhañjana-kāraṇa-karuṇaṁ
taṁ praṇamāmi ca śrī-śacī-tanayam [2]
```

**Translation:**  
Within Him swell ecstatic emotions that cause His throat to choke up with tears of love; the mighty resonance of His roar chastises all impious adversaries, and His causeless mercy dispels every fear of mundane transmigration. Unto that son of Śrī Śacīdevī, I offer my humble obeisances.

---

### Verse 3

```
aruṇāmbara-dhara-cāru-kapolaṁ
indu-vinindita-nakha-caya-ruciram
jalpita-nija-guṇa-nāma-vinodaṁ
taṁ praṇamāmi ca śrī-śacī-tanayam [3]
```

**Translation:**  
He is dressed in garments the color of the dawn; His cheeks are exceedingly captivating, and the brilliance of His nails puts the autumn moon to shame. He finds ecstatic pleasure in continually chanting the glories of His own holy names and pastimes. Unto that son of Śrī Śacīdevī, I offer my humble obeisances.

---

### Verse 4

```
vigalita-nayana-kamala-jaladhāraṁ
bhūṣaṇa-nava-rasa-bhāva-vikāram
gati-ati-manthara-nṛtya-vilāsaṁ
taṁ praṇamāmi ca śrī-śacī-tanayam [4]
```

**Translation:**  
Torrents of ecstatic tears stream ceaselessly from His blooming lotus eyes; He is ornamented with the bodily transformations of ever-fresh spiritual mellows, dancing in a slow, exquisitely graceful and majestic gait. Unto that son of Śrī Śacīdevī, I offer my humble obeisances.

---

### Verse 5

```
cañcala-cāru-caraṇa-gati-ruciraṁ
mañjīra-rañjita-pada-yuga-madhuram
candra-vinindita-śītala-vadanaṁ
taṁ praṇamāmi ca śrī-śacī-tanayam [5]
```

**Translation:**  
The nimble and lively steps of His lotus feet are wonderfully captivating; those feet are sweetened by melodious jingling ankle-bells, and His tranquil face outshines the cooling splendor of the full moon. Unto that son of Śrī Śacīdevī, I offer my humble obeisances.

---

### Verse 6

```
dhṛta-kaṭi-ḍora-kamaṇḍalu-daṇḍaṁ
divya-kalevara-muṇḍita-muṇḍam
durjana-kalmaṣa-khaṇḍana-daṇḍaṁ
taṁ praṇamāmi ca śrī-śacī-tanayam [6]
```

**Translation:**  
Accepting the order of sannyāsa, He wears the waist-cord and holds the water-pot and mendicant's staff; His divine form is graced with a shaved head, and His spiritual discipline smashes to pieces the sins of the wicked. Unto that son of Śrī Śacīdevī, I offer my humble obeisances.

---

### Verse 7

```
bhūṣaṇa-bhūraja-alakāvalitaṁ
kampita-bimbādhara-vara-ruciram
malayaja-viracita-ujjvala-tilakaṁ
taṁ praṇamāmi ca śrī-śacī-tanayam [7]
```

**Translation:**  
Decorated with the sacred dust of the earth, His face is framed by curling black tresses; His exquisite lips, red like ripe bimba fruit, tremble with divine fervor, and His forehead is graced with radiant sandalwood tilaka. Unto that son of Śrī Śacīdevī, I offer my humble obeisances.

---

### Verse 8

```
nindita-aruṇa-kamala-dala-nayanaṁ
ā-jānu-lambita-śrī-bhuja-yugalam
kalevara-kaiśora-nartaka-veṣaṁ
taṁ praṇamāmi ca śrī-śacī-tanayam [8]
```

**Translation:**  
His long, blossoming eyes eclipse the delicate petals of the reddish morning lotus; His divine arms extend gracefully all the way to His knees, and His youthful body is dressed in the magnificent style of an expert actor-dancer. Unto that son of Śrī Śacīdevī, I offer my humble obeisances.

## 5. Śrī Śrī Rādhā-kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-stotram
**Source:** *Ūrdhvāmnāya-tantra* (Spoken by Lord Śiva)  
**Refrain:**  
```
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam
```  
*(“O Śrīmatī Rādhikā, when, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?”)*

---

### Verse 1

```
munīndra-vṛnda-vandite triloka-śoka-hāriṇi
prasanna-vaktra-paṅkaje nikuñja-bhū-vilāsini
vrajendra-bhānu-nandini vrajendra-sūnu-saṅgate
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam [1]
```

**Translation:**  
O Goddess revered by the assemblies of foremost sages! O You who dispel the agony of all the three worlds! O You whose smiling countenance is like a blossoming lotus, sporting in the secluded groves of Vraja! O beloved daughter of King Vṛṣabhānu, eternal companion of the Prince of Vraja! When, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?

---

### Verse 2

```
aśoka-vṛkṣa-vallarī-vitāna-maṇḍapa-sthite
pravāla-jvāla-pallava-prabhāruṇāṅghri-komale
varābhaya-sphurat-kare prabhūta-sampad-ālaye
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam [2]
```

**Translation:**  
O Goddess gracefully seated in a bower canopy woven of blossoming creepers over aśoka branches! O You whose tender feet radiate the crimson blush of freshly sprouted coral leaves! O You whose upraised hand radiates absolute fearlessness and boons, the supreme treasury of all spiritual riches! When, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?

---

### Verse 3

```
anaṅga-raṅga-maṅgala-prasaṅga-bhaṅgura-bhruvāṁ
su-vibhramaṁ sa-sambhramaṁ dṛg-anta-bāṇa-pātanaiḥ
nirantaraṁ vaśī-kṛta-pratīta-nanda-nandane
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam [3]
```

**Translation:**  
Upon the auspicious stage of love-sports, with the playful curving of Your eyebrows, You shoot the piercing arrows of side-glances from the corners of Your eyes, continuously captivating and holding the famous son of Nanda completely under Your sweet spell. When, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?

---

### Verse 4

```
taḍit-suvarṇa-campaka-pradīpta-gaura-vigrahe
mukha-prabhā-parāsta-koṭi-śāradendu-maṇḍale
vicitra-citra-sañcarac-cakora-śāva-locane
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam [4]
```

**Translation:**  
Your fair transcendental form radiates with the golden brilliance of lightning, molten gold, and campaka blossoms! The beauty of Your face easily eclipses millions of autumn moons, and Your restless, artistic eyes dart like tender young cakora birds. When, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?

---

### Verse 5

```
madonmadāti-yauvane pramoda-māna-maṇḍite
priyānurāga-rañjite kalā-vilāsa-paṇḍite
ananya-dhanya-kuñja-rājya-kāma-keli-kovide
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam [5]
```

**Translation:**  
You are overflowing with blossoming eternal youth, beautifully adorned with playful lovers' indignation (*māna*); imbued with deep attachment for Your Beloved, You are the master of all fine arts and the sovereign ruler of love-sports within the uniquely blessed bowers of Vṛndāvana. When, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?

---

### Verse 6

```
aśeṣa-hāva-bhāva-dhīra-hīra-hāra-bhūṣite
prabhūta-śātakumbha-kumbha-kumbhi-kumbha-sustani
praśasta-manda-hāsya-cūrṇa-pūrṇa-saukhya-sāgare
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam [6]
```

**Translation:**  
Adorned with the diamond necklace of countless ecstatic expressions and transcendental gravity, endowed with bodily perfection, Your sweet gentle smile fills the ocean of divine happiness with fragrant pollen. When, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?

---

### Verse 7

```
mṛṇāla-vāla-vallarī-taraṅga-raṅga-dor-late
latāgra-lāsya-lola-nīla-locanāvalokane
lalal-lulan-milan-manojña-mugdha-mohināśrite
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam [7]
```

**Translation:**  
Your soft arms sway gracefully in the dance like fresh lotus creepers dancing upon waves; the restless, dark-blue corners of Your eyes dart like the tender tips of vines, and with Your enchanting sweetness You totally captivate Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the enchanter of Cupid Himself. When, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?

---

### Verse 8

```
suvarṇa-mālikāñcita-trirekha-kambu-kaṇṭhage
tri-sūtra-maṅgalī-guṇa-tri-ratna-dīpti-dīdhite
salola-nīla-kuntala-prasūna-guccha-gumphite
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam [8]
```

**Translation:**  
Your graceful neck, marked with three lines like a smooth conch-shell, is adorned with radiant golden necklaces and the brilliant three-jeweled auspicious thread; Your waving dark tresses are interwoven with fragrant bunches of fresh forest flowers. When, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?

---

### Verse 9

```
nitamba-bimba-lambamāna-puṣpa-mekhalā-guṇe
praśasta-ratna-kiṅkiṇī-kalāpa-madhya-mañjule
karīndra-śuṇḍa-daṇḍikā-varoha-saubhagoruke
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam [9]
```

**Translation:**  
Your shapely hips are graced with dangling girdles of flowers and belts of tinkling jeweled bells; Your beautiful thighs possess the exquisite tapering elegance of the trunk of a royal elephant. When, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?

---

### Verse 10

```
aneka-mantra-nāda-mañju-nūpurārava-skhalat
samāja-rāja-haṁsa-vaṁśa-nikvaṇāti-gaurave
vilola-hema-vallarī-viḍambi-cāru-caṅkrame
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam [10]
```

**Translation:**  
The sweet chime of Your ankle-bells sounds like the resonance of sacred mantras, completely surpassing the melodious calls of royal swans; Your graceful, flowing stride rivals the gentle trembling of a golden vine swaying in the breeze. When, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?

---

### Verse 11

```
ananta-koṭi-viṣṇu-loka-namra-padmajārcite
himādrijā-pulomajā-viriñcijā-vara-prade
apāra-siddhi-ṛddhi-digdha-sat-padāṅgulī-nakhe
kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam [11]
```

**Translation:**  
Lord Brahmā, before whom millions of Vaikuṇṭha worlds bow down, reverently worships You; You bestow boons upon Pārvatī, Indrāṇī, and Sarasvatī, and the radiant toenails of Your divine lotus feet are anointed with boundless mystic perfections. When, oh when, will You make me the recipient of Your merciful sidelong glance?

---

### Verse 12

```
makheśvari kriyeśvari svadheśvari sureśvari
triveda-bhāratīśvari pramāṇa-śāsaneśvari
rameśvari kṣameśvari pramoda-kānaneśvari
vrajeśvari vrajādhipe śrī-rādhike namo ’stu te [12]
```

**Translation:**  
You are the Mistress of sacred sacrifice, of all pious deeds, of ancestral oblations, and Queen of heaven; You are the embodiment of the threefold Vedas, the ultimate scriptural authority, the Empress above Lakṣmī, the mistress of patience, the Queen of the forest of bliss, and the Supreme Sovereign of Vraja! O Śrī Rādhikā, I offer my humble obeisances unto You!

---

### Verse 13

```
itīmam adbhutaṁ stavaṁ niśamya bhānu-nandinī
karotu santataṁ janaṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam
bhavet tadaiva sañcita-tri-rūpa-karma-nāśanaṁ
labhet tadā vrajendra-sūnu-maṇḍala-praveśanam [13]
```

**Translation:**  
Upon hearing this wondrous prayer, may Śrī Vṛṣabhānu-nandinī perpetually bestow Her merciful sidelong glance upon that person! At that very instant, all three categories of accumulated karma are completely obliterated, and that fortunate soul enters into the eternal assembly of Śrī Kṛṣṇa's confidential associates.

---

### Verses 14–16 (Phala-śruti)

```
rākāyāṁ ca sitāṣṭamyāṁ daśamyāṁ ca viśuddha-dhīḥ
ekādaśyāṁ trayodaśyāṁ yaḥ paṭhet sādhakaḥ sudhīḥ [14]

yaṁ yaṁ kāmayate kāmaṁ taṁ taṁ prāpnoti durlabham
rādhā-kṛpā-kaṭākṣeṇa bhaktis syāt prema-lakṣaṇā [15]

ūrdhvāmnāye stutaṁ stotram yaḥ paṭhet prayato naraḥ
sa kṛpā-pātra-tāṁ yāti rādhā-mādhava-padayoḥ [16]
```

**Translation:**  
Whoever, possessing purified consciousness, recites this stotram on the full-moon day, the eighth, tenth, eleventh (Ekādaśī), or thirteenth day of the waxing moon, easily attains the fulfillment of their deepest spiritual desires. By the merciful sidelong glance of Śrī Rādhā, they are granted pure ecstatic love (*prema-bhakti*) at the lotus feet of Śrī Śrī Rādhā-Mādhava.

## 6. Śrī Nanda-nandanāṣṭakam
**Meter:** Traditional Sanskrit devotional octet  
**Refrain:**  
```
namāmi nanda-nandanam
```  
*(“I offer my respectful obeisances unto the delightful son of Nanda.”)*

---

### Verse 1

```
sucāru-vaktra-maṇḍalaṁ sukarṇa-ratna-kuṇḍalam
sucarcitāṅga-candanaṁ namāmi nanda-nandanam [1]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the son of Nanda, whose rounded face is exquisitely enchanting, whose ears are adorned with dazzling jeweled earrings, and whose transcendental limbs are lovingly smeared with cooling sandalwood paste.

---

### Verse 2

```
sudīrgha-netra-paṅkajaṁ śikhi-śikhaṇḍa-mūrdhajam
anaṅga-koṭi-mohanaṁ namāmi nanda-nandanam [2]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the son of Nanda, whose elongated lotus eyes are supremely captivating, whose dark hair is crowned with an iridescent peacock feather, and whose beauty enchants millions of Cupids.

---

### Verse 3

```
sunāsikāgra-mauktikaṁ svacchanda-danta-paṅktikam
navāmbudāṅga-cikkaṇaṁ namāmi nanda-nandanam [3]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the son of Nanda, from the tip of whose graceful nose swings a gleaming pearl, whose teeth shine with radiant luster, and whose silken complexion glistens like a fresh monsoon raincloud.

---

### Verse 4

```
kareṇa veṇu-rañjitaṁ gati-karīndra-gañjitam
dukūla-pīta-śobhanaṁ namāmi nanda-nandanam [4]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the son of Nanda, whose lotus hands play the melodious flute, whose royal gait surpasses that of an impassioned elephant, and whose dark limbs are beautifully adorned in glistening yellow silks.

---

### Verse 5

```
tri-bhaṅga-deha-sundaraṁ nakha-dyuti-sudhākaram
amūlya-ratna-bhūṣaṇaṁ namāmi nanda-nandanam [5]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the son of Nanda, standing in the supremely charming threefold-bending posture (*tri-bhaṅga*), the luster of whose toenails eclipses the full moon, and who is bedecked with priceless jewels.

---

### Verse 6

```
sugandha-aṅga-saurabhaṁ uro-virāji-kaustubham
sphurac-chrīvatsa-lāñchanaṁ namāmi nanda-nandanam [6]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the son of Nanda, whose limbs diffuse an intoxicating celestial aroma, whose chest is brilliantly adorned with the Kaustubha jewel and the glorious mark of Śrīvatsa.

---

### Verse 7

```
vṛndāvana-sunāgaraṁ vilāsānuga-vāsasam
surendra-garva-mocanaṁ namāmi nanda-nandanam [7]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the son of Nanda, the consummate hero of Vṛndāvana attired in dress perfect for playful pastimes, who humbled and pulverized the arrogant pride of Indra.

---

### Verse 8

```
vrajāṅganā-sunāyakaṁ sadā sukha-pradāyakam
jagan-manaḥ-pralobhanaṁ namāmi nanda-nandanam [8]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the son of Nanda, the dearmost hero of the gopīs of Vraja, who constantly showers them with transcendental ecstasy and whose incomparable beauty enchants every mind in creation.

---

### Verse 9 (Phala-śruti)

```
śrī-nanda-nandanāṣṭakaṁ paṭhed yaḥ śraddhayānvitaḥ
tared bhavābdhiṁ dustaraṁ labhet tad-aṅghri-yugmakam [9]
```

**Translation:**  
Whoever recites this Śrī Nanda-Nandanāṣṭakam with deep faith and devotion will easily cross over the seemingly impassable ocean of material existence and attain eternal residence at the divine lotus feet of Lord Kṛṣṇa.

## 7. Śrī Dāmodarāṣṭakam
**Source:** *Padma Purāṇa* (Spoken by Satyavrata Muni)  
**The Primary Hymn of Kārtika Month**

---

### Verse 1

```
namāmīśvaraṁ sac-cid-ānanda-rūpaṁ
lasat-kuṇḍalaṁ gokule bhrājamānam
yaśodā-bhiyolūkhalād dhāvamānaṁ
parāmṛṣṭam atyantato drutya gopyā [1]
```

**Translation:**  
Unto the Supreme Lord, whose form is the embodiment of eternity, knowledge, and bliss, whose shark-shaped earrings playfully swing and shine, and who radiates splendidly in Gokula; who, in fear of Mother Yaśodā, fled from the grinding mortar but was caught from behind as she ran swiftly after Him — unto that Supreme Lord, I offer my humble obeisances.

---

### Verse 2

```
rudantaṁ muhur netra-yugmaṁ mṛjantaṁ
karāmbhoja-yugmena sātaṅka-netram
muhuḥ śvāsa-kampa-tri-rekhāṅka-kaṇṭha-
sthita-graiva-dāmodaraṁ bhakti-baddham [2]
```

**Translation:**  
Crying repeatedly and rubbing both His frightened eyes with His two lotus hands, His neck—adorned with three lines like a conch shell—quivers from quick breathing, shaking the pearl necklace upon His chest. I bow to that Lord Dāmodara, whose belly is bound not by mere ropes, but by the pure love of His devotee.

---

### Verse 3

```
itīdṛk sva-līlābhir ānanda-kuṇḍe
sva-ghoṣaṁ nimajjantam ākhyāpayantam
tadīyeśita-jñeṣu bhaktair jitatvaṁ
punaḥ prematas taṁ śatāvṛtti vande [3]
```

**Translation:**  
By such childhood pastimes He plunges the cowherd settlement of Gokula into pools of transcendental ecstasy, demonstrating to those absorbed in knowledge of His majesty that He is conquered only by pure intimate love. Hundreds and hundreds of times, with deepest affection, I offer my praises unto that Lord Dāmodara.

---

### Verse 4

```
varaṁ deva mokṣaṁ na mokṣāvadhiṁ vā
na cānyaṁ vṛṇe 'haṁ vareśād apīha
idaṁ te vapur nātha gopāla-bālaṁ
sadā me manasy āvirāstāṁ kim anyaiḥ [4]
```

**Translation:**  
O Lord, although You are able to grant every boon, I do not seek impersonal liberation, nor the highest summit of salvation in Vaikuṇṭha, nor any other reward. My only longing, O Lord, is that this form of Yours as the child cowherd boy of Gokula may ever remain manifest within my heart. What use have I for any other gift?

---

### Verse 5

```
idaṁ te mukhāmbhojam atyanta-nīlair
vṛtaṁ kuntalaiḥ snigdha-raktaiś ca gopyā
muhuś cumbitaṁ bimba-raktādharaṁ me
manasy āvirāstām alaṁ lakṣa-lābhaiḥ [5]
```

**Translation:**  
Your lotus face is framed by soft, dark curling locks tinged with red, and Mother Yaśodā showers kisses upon Your lips, which are as crimson as the bimba fruit. May this vision of Your lotus face always remain clearly manifest within my heart. Of what value are hundreds of thousands of other achievements to me?

---

### Verse 6

```
namo deva dāmodarānanta viṣṇo
prasīda prabho duḥkha-jālābdhi-magnam
kṛpā-dṛṣṭi-vṛṣṭyāti-dīnaṁ batānu-
gṛhāṇeśa mām ajñam edhy akṣi-dṛśyaḥ [6]
```

**Translation:**  
O Supreme Lord! O Dāmodara! O Infinite One! O Viṣṇu! Be pleased with me, my Master! I am sinking helplessly in an ocean of worldly suffering. Shower upon me the rain of Your merciful glance, deliver this ignorant soul, and become visible before my eyes!

---

### Verse 7

```
kuberātmajau baddha-mūrtyaiva yadvat
tvayā mocitau bhakti-bhājau kṛtau ca
tathā prema-bhaktiṁ svakāṁ me prayaccha
na mokṣe graho me 'sti dāmodareha [7]
```

**Translation:**  
Just as You liberated the two sons of Kuvera (Nalakūvara and Maṇigrīva) from their curse as twin arjuna trees even while You were bound to the mortar, awarding them the treasure of pure devotion, please likewise bestow upon me Your own prema-bhakti! I have no desire for mere liberation, O Lord Dāmodara.

---

### Verse 8

```
namas te 'stu dāmne sphurad-dīpti-dhāmne
tvadīyodarāyātha viśvasya dhāmne
namo rādhikāyai tvadīya-priyāyai
namo 'nanta-līlāya devāya tubhyam [8]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the effulgent rope that binds Your abdomen, and unto Your sacred belly, which is the resting place of the entire cosmos! I offer my respectful obeisances unto Śrīmatī Rādhikā, Your supreme beloved, and unto You, the Supreme Personality of Godhead, who manifest infinite divine pastimes!

## 8. Lamp Offering (Dīpa-dāna) to Śrī Śrī Rādhikā-Keśava Rāya Jī 🪔

### Mantras and Prayer for Lamp Offering

While offering the ghee lamp (*dīpa-dāna*) during the holy month of Kārtika, devotees meditate upon the transcendental form of Śrī Śrī Rādhikā and Keśava Rāya:

> **śrī-kṛṣṇa rādhikā-kānta kṛpā-sindho jagat-pate**  
> **gopeśa gopikā-kānta rādhā-kānta namo 'stu te**  
>  
> *"I offer my respectful obeisances unto Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the beloved of Rādhikā, ocean of mercy, Lord of the universe, master of the cowherds and gopīs, the dear lover of Rādhā!"*

```
dāmodarāṣṭakaṁ kṛtvā dīpa-dānaṁ karomi te
rādhikā-sahitaṁ vande keśavaṁ kleśa-nāśanam
```

**Translation:**  
Having chanted the Dāmodarāṣṭakam, with deep love and devotion I offer this sacred ghee lamp unto You, O Lord Dāmodara! Accompanied by Śrīmatī Rādhikā, I revere Śrī Keśava Rāya, who destroys all material misery and bestows pure ecstatic love.
