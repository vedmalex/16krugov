# ISKCON Morning Service (Verses & Translation)
### Daily Morning Program (Maṅgala-ārati, Japa, Darśana)

> Collection of devotional songs and prayers recited daily during the ISKCON morning program: Śrī Gurvāṣṭaka, Vibhāvarī Śeṣa, Namaste Narasiṁhāya, Śrī Tulasī-pūjā, Ten Offenses to the Holy Name, Śrī Vaiṣṇava-praṇāma, Śrī Śikṣāṣṭaka, and Śrī Prema-dhvani.

## 1. Śrī Gurvāṣṭaka
**Author / Source:** *Śrīla Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura*

### Verse 1

```
saṁsāra-dāvānala-līḍha-loka-
trāṇāya kāruṇya-ghanāghanatvam
prāptasya kalyāṇa-guṇārṇavasya
vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam [1]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru, who is an ocean of auspicious qualities and who has attained the nature of a raincloud of mercy to deliver the people devoured by the blazing forest fire of material existence.

---

### Verse 2

```
mahāprabhoḥ kīrtana-nṛtya-gīta-
vāditra-mādyan-manaso rasena
romāñca-kampāśru-taraṅga-bhājo
vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam [2]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru, whose mind is intoxicated by the mellows of Lord Mahāprabhu’s kīrtana, dancing, singing, and playing of musical instruments, and who experiences waves of hair standing on end, trembling, and tears.

---

### Verse 3

```
śrī-vigrahārādhana-nitya-nānā-
śṛṅgāra-tan-mandira-mārjanādau
yuktasya bhaktāṁś ca niyuñjato 'pi
vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam [3]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru, who is always engaged in the daily worship of the Deity with various decorations, cleaning His temple, and other such services, and who also engages the devotees in this worship.

---

### Verse 4

```
catur-vidha-śrī-bhagavat-prasāda-
svādv-anna-tṛptān hari-bhakta-saṅghān
kṛtvaiva tṛptiṁ bhajataḥ sadaiva
vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam [4]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru, who always feels satisfaction only upon fully satisfying the assemblies of devotees of Lord Hari with the four kinds of delicious foodstuffs that are the prasāda of Lord Kṛṣṇa.

---

### Verse 5

```
śrī-rādhikā-mādhavayor apāra-
mādhurya-līlā guṇa-rūpa-nāmnām
prati-kṣaṇāsvādana-lolupasya
vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam [5]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru, who is always eagerly hankering to relish at every moment the boundless sweetness of the pastimes, qualities, forms, and names of Śrī Rādhikā and Mādhava.

---

### Verse 6

```
nikuñja-yūno rati-keli-siddhyai
yā yālibhir yuktir apekṣaṇīyā
tatrāti-dākṣyād ati-vallabhasya
vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam [6]
```

**Translation:**  
The spiritual master is very dear, because he is expert in assisting the gopīs, who at different times make different tasteful arrangements for the perfection of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa’s conjugal loving affairs within the groves of Vṛndāvana. I offer my most respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of such a spiritual master.

---

### Verse 7

```
sākṣād-dharitvena samasta-śāstrair
uktas tathā bhāvyata eva sadbhiḥ
kintu prabhor yaḥ priya eva tasya
vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam [7]
```

**Translation:**  
The guru is proclaimed by all revealed scriptures to be directly Śrī Hari, and is accepted as such by saintly authorities, yet he is the most dear servitor of the Lord. I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of that Śrī Guru.

---

### Verse 8

```
yasya prasādād bhagavat-prasādo
yasyāprasādān na gatiḥ kuto 'pi
dhyāyan stuvaṁs tasya yaśas tri-sandhyaṁ
vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam [8]
```

**Translation:**  
By the mercy of the guru one attains the mercy of Lord Kṛṣṇa; without his mercy there is no shelter anywhere. Meditating upon and praising his glories three times a day, I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru.

---

## 2. Vibhāvarī Śeṣa
**Author / Source:** *Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura (Kalyāṇa-kalpataru)*

### Verse 1

```
vibhāvarī śeṣa, āloka-praveśa,
nidrā chāṛi' uṭho jīva
bolo hari hari, mukunda murāri,
rāma kṛṣṇa hayagrīva [1]
```

**Translation:**  
The night has ended and the light of dawn is entering. Give up your sleep and arise, O jīva! Chant the holy names of Lord Hari: Mukunda, Murāri, Rāma, Kṛṣṇa, and Hayagrīva!

---

### Verse 2

```
nṛsiṁha vāmana, śrī-madhusūdana,
brajendra-nandana śyāma
pūtanā-ghātana, kaiṭabha-śātana,
jaya dāśarathi-rāma [2]
```

**Translation:**  
Lord Nṛsiṁha, Lord Vāmana, Śrī Madhusūdana, the dark-complexioned son of the King of Vraja, the killer of Pūtanā, and the destroyer of Kaiṭabha—all glories to Lord Rāma, the son of King Daśaratha!

---

### Verse 3

```
yaśodā dulāla, govinda-gopāla,
vṛndāvana purandara
gopī-priya-jana, rādhikā-ramaṇa,
bhuvana-sundara-bara [3]
```

**Translation:**  
He is the darling son of Yaśodā, Govinda, Gopāla, the monarch of Vṛndāvana, the beloved of the gopīs, the lover of Rādhikā, and the most handsome person in all the worlds.

---

### Verse 4

```
rāvaṇāntakara, mākhana-taskara,
gopī-jana-vastra-hārī
brajera rākhāla, gopa-vṛnda-pāla,
citta-hārī baṁśī-dhārī [4]
```

**Translation:**  
He is the slayer of Rāvaṇa, the thief of butter, the stealer of the gopīs’ garments, the cowherd boy of Vraja, the protector of the cowherd boys, the stealer of hearts, and the holder of the flute.

---

### Verse 5

```
yogīndra-vandana, śrī-nanda-nandana,
braja-jana-bhaya-hārī
navīna nīrada, rūpa manohara,
mohana-baṁśī-bihārī [5]
```

**Translation:**  
He is worshiped by the best of yogīs and is the son of Nanda. He removes all the fears of the inhabitants of Vraja. He is the color of a fresh rain cloud, and His form is enchanting. He wanders about, playing His enchanting flute.

---

### Verse 6

```
yaśodā-nandana, kaṁsa-nisūdana,
nikuñja-rāsa-vilāsī
kadamba-kānana, rāsa-parāyaṇa,
bṛnda-vipina-nivāsī [6]
```

**Translation:**  
He is the beloved son of Yaśodā, the slayer of Kaṁsa, the enjoyer of the rāsa dance in the forest bowers, devoted to the rāsa dance in the kadamba groves, and the resident of the forest of Vṛndāvana.

---

### Verse 7

```
ānanda-vardhana, prema-niketana,
phula-śara-yojaka kāma
gopāṅganā-gaṇa, citta-vinodana,
samasta-guṇa-gaṇa-dhāma [7]
```

**Translation:**  
He increases transcendental bliss, is the abode of pure love, and is the transcendental Cupid who shoots flower-arrows. He delights the hearts of the cowherd maidens and is the reservoir of all transcendental qualities.

---

### Verse 8

```
jāmuna-jīvana, keli-parāyaṇa,
mānasa-candra-cakora
nāma-sudhā-rasa, gāo kṛṣṇa-jaśa
rākho vacana mana mora [8]
```

**Translation:**  
He is the life of the River Yamunā, ever absorbed in playful pastimes, and the cakora bird drinking the moonlight of the devotees’ minds. O my mind, heed my words: sing the glories of Lord Kṛṣṇa and relish the nectarean mellow of the holy name.

---

## 3. Namaste Narasiṁhāya
**Author / Source:** *Nṛsiṁha praṇāma (Caitanya-caritāmṛta 16.52–53; Gīta-govinda, Daśāvatāra-stotra 4)*

### Verse 1

```
namas te nara-siṁhāya
prahlādāhlāda-dāyine
hiraṇyakaśipor vakṣaḥ-
śilā-ṭaṅka-nakhālaye [1]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my obeisances to Lord Nṛsiṁha, who gives joy to Prahlāda Mahārāja and whose nails are like chisels on the stonelike chest of the demon Hiraṇyakaśipu.

---

### Verse 2

```
ito nṛsiṁhaḥ parato nṛsiṁho
yato yato yāmi tato nṛsiṁhaḥ
bahir nṛsiṁho hṛdaye nṛsiṁho
nṛsiṁham ādiṁ śaraṇaṁ prapadye [2]
```

**Translation:**  
Lord Nṛsiṁha is here and He is also there. Wherever I go Lord Nṛsiṁha is there. He is in the heart and is outside as well. I surrender to Lord Nṛsiṁha, the origin of all things and the supreme refuge.

---

### Verse 3

```
tava kara-kamala-vare nakham adbhuta-śṛṅgaṁ
dalita-hiraṇyakaśipu-tanu-bhṛṅgam
keśava dhṛta-nara-hari-rūpa jaya jagadīśa hare
jaya jagadīśa hare jaya jagadīśa hare [3]
```

**Translation:**  
O Keśava! O Lord of the universe! O Lord Hari, who have assumed the form of half-man, half-lion! All glories to You! Just as one can easily crush a wasp between one’s fingernails, so in the same way the body of the wasp-like demon Hiraṇyakaśipu has been ripped apart by the wonderful pointed nails on Your beautiful lotus hands.

---

## 4. Tulasī-pūjā
**Author / Source:** *Śrī Tulasī praṇāma, ārati (Kṛṣṇa Dāsa), pradakṣiṇa-mantra*

### Verse 1

```
vṛndāyai tulasī-devyai priyāyai keśavasya ca
viṣṇu-bhakti-prade devī satyavatyai namo namaḥ [1]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my repeated obeisances unto Vṛndā, Śrīmatī Tulasī-devī, who is very dear to Lord Keśava. O goddess, you bestow devotional service to Lord Viṣṇu and possess the highest truth.

---

### Verse 2

```
namo namaḥ tulasī kṛṣṇa-preyasi namo namaḥ
rādhā-kṛṣṇa-sevā pābo ei abilāṣī [2]
```

**Translation:**  
O Tulasī, beloved of Lord Kṛṣṇa, I bow down to you again and again. My desire is to obtain the service of Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Lord Kṛṣṇa.

---

### Verse 3

```
ye tomāra śaraṇa loy, tara vāñchā pūrṇa hoy
kṛpā kori' koro tāre vṛndāvana-vāsi [3]
```

**Translation:**  
Whoever takes shelter of you has his desires fulfilled. Bestowing your mercy upon him, you make him a resident of Vṛndāvana.

---

### Verse 4

```
mora ei abhilāṣa, vilāsa kuñje dio vāsa
nayana heribo sadā yugala-rūpa-rāśi [4]
```

**Translation:**  
This is my desire: please grant me residence in the pleasure groves, so that with my eyes I may always behold the boundless beauty of the Divine Couple, Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.

---

### Verse 5

```
ei nivedana dhara, sakhīra anugata koro
sevā-adhikāra diye koro nīja dāsī [5]
```

**Translation:**  
Please accept this prayer: make me a follower of the sakhīs, and granting me the qualification for devotional service, make me your own maidservant.

---

### Verse 6

```
dīna kṛṣṇa-dāse koy, ei yena mora hoy
śrī-rādhā-govinda-preme sadā yena bhāsi [6]
```

**Translation:**  
The fallen Kṛṣṇadāsa prays, “May this be mine, that I may always swim in the love of Śrī Rādhā and Govinda.”

---

### Verse 7

```
yāni kāni ca pāpāni brahma-hatyādikāni ca
tāni tāni praṇaśyanti pradakṣiṇaḥ pade pade [7]
```

**Translation:**  
Whatever sins one may have committed, even the killing of a brāhmaṇa, are destroyed at every step of circumambulating Śrīmatī Tulasī-devī.

---

## 5. Ten Offenses to the Holy Name
**Author / Source:** *Padma Purāṇa (Brahma-khaṇḍa 25.15–18, 22–23; Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 11.521–526)*

### The Ten Offenses to the Holy Name (from Padma Purāṇa)

1. To blaspheme the devotees who have dedicated their lives for propagating the holy name of the Lord.
2. To consider the names of demigods like Lord Śiva or Lord Brahmā to be equal to, or independent of, the name of Lord Viṣṇu.
3. To disobey the orders of the spiritual master.
4. To blaspheme the Vedic literature or literature in pursuance of the Vedic version.
5. To consider the glories of chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa to be imagination.
6. To give some interpretation on the holy name of the Lord.
7. To commit sinful activities on the strength of the holy name of the Lord.
8. To consider chanting of Hare Kṛṣṇa one of the auspicious ritualistic activities offered in the Vedas as fruitive activities (karma-kāṇḍa).
9. To instruct a faithless person about the glories of the holy name.
10. To not have complete faith in the chanting of the holy names and to maintain material attachments, even after understanding so many instructions on this matter.

*It is also an offense to be inattentive while chanting.*  
*Every devotee who claims to be a Vaiṣṇava must guard against these offenses in order to quickly achieve the desired success — pure love for Lord Kṛṣṇa.*


---

### Verses from Padma Purāṇa

### Verse 1

```
satāṁ nindā nāmnaḥ paramam aparādhaṁ vitanute
yataḥ khyātiṁ yātaṁ katham u sahate tad-vigarhām [1]
```

**Translation:**  
Blasphemy of saintly persons constitutes the foremost offense against harināma. How indeed could the holy name tolerate the vilification of those through whom it has attained fame?

---

### Verse 2

```
śivasya śrī-viṣṇor ya iha guṇa-nāmādi-sakalaṁ
dhiyā bhinnaṁ paśyet sa khalu hari-nāmāhita-karaḥ [2]
```

**Translation:**  
In this world, whoever considers the names, qualities, and everything else of Lord Śiva to be separate from those of Śrī Viṣṇu is certainly an offender against harināma.

---

### Verse 3

```
guror avajñā śruti-śāstra-nindanaṁ
tathārtha-vādo hari-nāmni kalpanam [3]
```

**Translation:**  
To disobey the guru, to blaspheme the Vedic scriptures, to consider the glories of harināma to be an exaggeration, and to give a concocted interpretation to harināma.

---

### Verse 4

```
nāmno balād yasya hi pāpa-buddhir
na vidyate tasya yamair hi śuddhiḥ [4]
```

**Translation:**  
For one who maintains a sinful mentality on the strength of the holy name, there is certainly no purification, even by all the rules and disciplines of restraint.

---

### Verse 5

```
dharma-vrata-tyāga-hutādi-sarva-
śubha-kriyā-sāmyam api pramādaḥ [5]
```

**Translation:**  
To consider harināma to be equal to all auspicious pious activities, such as dharma, vows, renunciation, and sacrifice, is an offense.

---

### Verse 6

```
aśraddadhāne vimukhe 'py aśṛṇvati
yaś copadeśaḥ śiva-nāmāparādhaḥ [6]
```

**Translation:**  
To instruct one who is faithless, averse, or unwilling to listen is an offense against the auspicious holy name.

---

### Verse 7

```
śrute 'pi nāma-māhātmye yaḥ prīti-rahito naraḥ
ahaṁ-mamādi-paramo nāmni so 'py aparādha-kṛt [7]
```

**Translation:**  
A person who, even after hearing the glories of the holy name, remains devoid of affection for it and is absorbed in the conceptions of "I" and "mine" is also an offender to the holy name.

---

### Verse 8

```
jāte nāmāparādhe tu pramāde tu kathañcana
sadā saṅkīrtayan-nāma tad-eka-śaraṇo bhavet [8]
```

**Translation:**  
If an offense to the holy name is somehow committed through negligence, one should constantly chant the holy name and take exclusive shelter of it.

---

### Verse 9

```
nāmāparādha-yuktānāṁ nāmāny eva haranty agham
aviśrānti-prayuktāni tāny evārtha-karāṇi ca [9]
```

**Translation:**  
For those who commit offenses to the holy name, the holy names alone destroy their sins, and when chanted continuously without cessation, those very names certainly bestow the ultimate goal.

---

## 6. Śrī Vaiṣṇava-praṇāma
**Author / Source:** *Traditional Vaiṣṇava praṇāma-mantra*

### Verse 1

```
vāñchā-kalpatarubhyaś ca kṛpā-sindhubhya eva ca
patitānāṁ pāvanebhyo vaiṣṇavebhyo namo namaḥ [1]
```

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto all the Vaiṣṇava devotees of the Lord. They are just like desire trees who can fulfill the desires of everyone, they are oceans of mercy, and they are the purifiers of all the fallen souls.

## 7. Śikṣāṣṭaka
**Author / Source:** *Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu*

### Verse 1

```
ceto-darpaṇa-mārjanaṁ bhava-mahā-dāvāgni-nirvāpaṇaṁ
śreyaḥ-kairava-candrikā-vitaraṇaṁ vidyā-vadhū-jīvanam
ānandāmbudhi-vardhanaṁ prati-padaṁ pūrṇāmṛtāsvādanaṁ
sarvātma-snapanaṁ paraṁ vijayate śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam [1]
```

**Translation:**  
Let supreme victory be unto the śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana, which cleanses the mirror of the heart, extinguishes the great forest fire of material existence, spreads the soothing moonbeams that awaken the white lotus of supreme good fortune, and is the very life of the bride of transcendental knowledge. It constantly swells the ocean of divine bliss, grants the taste of full nectar at every step, and completely bathes and purifies the entire self.

---

### Verse 2

```
nāmnām akāri bahudhā nija-sarva-śaktis
tatrārpitā niyamitaḥ smaraṇe na kālaḥ
etādṛśī tava kṛpā bhagavan mamāpi
durdaivam īdṛśam ihājani nānurāgaḥ [2]
```

**Translation:**  
O Lord, You have manifested Your holy names in manifold ways and invested all Your personal potencies in them, and there are no regulated times for remembering them. Such is Your mercy, O Supreme Lord, yet such is my misfortune that no attraction for harināma has arisen in me.

---

### Verse 3

```
tṛṇād api sunīcena taror iva sahiṣṇunā
amāninā mānadena kīrtanīyaḥ sadā hariḥ [3]
```

**Translation:**  
One should chant the holy name of the Lord in a humble state of mind, thinking oneself lower than the straw in the street; one should be more tolerant than a tree, devoid of all sense of false prestige, and should be ready to offer all respect to others. In such a state of mind one can chant the holy name of the Lord constantly.

---

### Verse 4

```
na dhanaṁ na janaṁ na sundarīṁ kavitāṁ vā jagad-īśa kāmaye
mama janmani janmanīśvare bhavatād bhaktir ahaitukī tvayi [4]
```

**Translation:**  
O Lord of the universe, I do not desire material wealth, nor do I desire beautiful women, nor do I want any number of followers. I only want Your causeless devotional service, birth after birth.

---

### Verse 5

```
ayi nanda-tanuja kiṅkaraṁ patitaṁ māṁ viṣame bhavāmbudhau
kṛpayā tava pāda-paṅkaja-sthita-dhūlī-sadṛśaṁ vicintaya [5]
```

**Translation:**  
O son of Nanda, Lord Kṛṣṇa, I am Your servant, fallen into this terrible ocean of material existence. Please, by Your mercy, consider me as a particle of dust at Your lotus feet.

---

### Verse 6

```
nayanaṁ galad-aśru-dhārayā vadanaṁ gadgada-ruddhayā girā
pulakair nicitaṁ vapuḥ kadā tava nāma-grahaṇe bhaviṣyati [6]
```

**Translation:**  
O my Lord, when will my eyes be decorated with tears of love flowing constantly when I chant Your holy name? When will my voice choke up, and when will the hairs of my body stand on end at the recitation of Your name?

---

### Verse 7

```
yugāyitaṁ nimeṣeṇa cakṣuṣā prāvṛṣāyitam
śūnyāyitaṁ jagat sarvaṁ govinda-virahena me [7]
```

**Translation:**  
My Lord Govinda, because of separation from You, I consider even a moment a great millennium. Tears flow from my eyes like torrents of rain, and I see the entire world as void.

---

### Verse 8

```
āśliṣya vā pāda-ratāṁ pinaṣṭu mām adarśanān marma-hatāṁ karotu vā
yathā tathā vā vidadhātu lampaṭo mat-prāṇa-nāthas tu sa eva nāparaḥ [8]
```

**Translation:**  
Let Lord Kṛṣṇa embrace me, who am devoted to His lotus feet, or trample me, or let Him break my heart by not being visible to me. That debauchee may act as He pleases, yet He alone, and no one else, is the Lord of my life.

---

## 8. Śrī Prema-dhvani
**Author / Source:** *Traditional ISKCON glorification prayers*

### Verse 1

```
jaya oṁ viṣṇu-pāda paramahaṁsa parivrājakācārya
aṣṭottara-śata śrī śrīmad a. c. bhaktivedānta svāmī mahārāja prabhupāda kī jaya!
iskkon saṁsthāpakācārya śrīla prabhupāda kī jaya!
jaya oṁ viṣṇu-pāda paramahaṁsa parivrājakācārya
aṣṭottara-śata śrī śrīmad bhaktisiddhānta sarasvatī gosvāmī mahārāja prabhupāda kī jaya! [1]
```

**Translation:**  
All glories to the ācārya Oṁ Viṣṇupāda Paramahaṁsa Parivrājakācārya Aṣṭottara-śata Śrī Śrīmad A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Mahārāja Prabhupāda, Founder-Ācārya of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness! All glories to Oṁ Viṣṇupāda Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Gosvāmī Mahārāja Prabhupāda!

---

### Verse 2

```
ananta-koṭi vaiṣṇava-vṛnda kī jaya!
nāmācārya śrīla haridāsa ṭhākura kī jaya! [2]
```

**Translation:**  
All glories to the unlimited millions of Vaiṣṇava devotees! All glories to the Master of the Holy Name, Śrīla Haridāsa Ṭhākura!

---

### Verse 3

```
prema-se kaho śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya prabhu-nityānanda
śrī-advaita gadādhara śrīvāsādi-gaura-bhakta-vṛnda kī jaya! [3]
```

**Translation:**  
Call out with pure love: All glories to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, Lord Nityānanda, Śrī Advaita, Gadādhara, Śrīvāsa, and all the associates of Lord Caitanya!

---

### Verse 4

```
śrī-śrī-rādhā-kṛṣṇa gopa-gopīnātha śyāma-kuṇḍa rādhā-kuṇḍa giri-govardhana kī jaya!
śrī vṛndāvana-dhāma kī jaya! śrī navadvīpa-māyāpura-dhāma kī jaya!
śrī jagannātha purī kṣetra-dhāma kī jaya! [4]
```

**Translation:**  
All glories to Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, the cowherd boys and maidens, Śyāma-kuṇḍa, Rādhā-kuṇḍa, and Giri Govardhana! All glories to holy Śrī Vṛndāvana-dhāma! All glories to Śrī Navadvīpa-Māyāpura-dhāma! All glories to Śrī Jagannātha Purī-dhāma!

---

### Verse 5

```
gaṅgā-māyī kī jaya! yamunā-māyī kī jaya!
tulasī-devī kī jaya! bhakti-devī kī jaya!
śrī hari-nāma saṅkīrtana-yajña kī jaya!
samaveta bhakta-vṛnda kī jaya!
gaura-premānande hari-haribol! [5]
```

**Translation:**  
All glories to Mother Gaṅgā! All glories to Mother Yamunā! All glories to Tulasī-devī! All glories to Bhakti-devī! All glories to the congregational chanting of the Holy Names — Śrī Hari-nāma Saṅkīrtana! All glories to the assembled devotees! In the ecstatic bliss of Lord Gaura chant: Hari-Haribol!
