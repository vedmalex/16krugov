## 1. Śrī Gurvāṣṭaka
**Author / Source:** *Śrīla Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura*

### Verse 1

<center>
saṁsāra-dāvānala-līḍha-loka-<br/>
trāṇāya kāruṇya-ghanāghanatvam<br/>
prāptasya kalyāṇa-guṇārṇavasya<br/>
vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam [1]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Of material existence <sub>saṁsāra</sub>, by the forest fire <sub>dāva-anala</sub>, afflicted <sub>līḍha</sub>, the people <sub>loka</sub>, for the deliverance <sub>trāṇāya</sub>, of mercy <sub>kāruṇya</sub>, the quality of a raincloud <sub>ghanāghana-tvam</sub>, of one who has attained <sub>prāptasya</sub>, auspicious <sub>kalyāṇa</sub>, of the ocean of qualities <sub>guṇa-arṇavasya</sub>, I offer obeisances <sub>vande</sub>, of the spiritual master <sub>guroḥ</sub>, unto the divine lotus feet <sub>śrī-caraṇa-aravindam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru, who is an ocean of auspicious qualities and who has attained the nature of a raincloud of mercy to deliver the people devoured by the blazing forest fire of material existence.

### Verse 2

<center>
mahāprabhoḥ kīrtana-nṛtya-gīta-<br/>
vāditra-mādyan-manaso rasena<br/>
romāñca-kampāśru-taraṅga-bhājo<br/>
vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam [2]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Of Lord Mahāprabhu <sub>mahāprabhoḥ</sub>, whose mind is exhilarated by the chanting <sub>kīrtana-nṛtya-gīta-vāditra-mādyan-manasaḥ</sub>, by the mellows of pure devotion <sub>rasena</sub>, who experiences the waves of standing hair <sub>romāñca-kampa-aśru-taraṅga-bhājaḥ</sub>, I offer my respectful obeisances <sub>vande</sub>, of my spiritual master <sub>guroḥ</sub>, unto the auspicious lotus feet <sub>śrī-caraṇa-aravindam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru, whose mind is intoxicated by the mellows of Lord Mahāprabhu’s kīrtana, dancing, singing, and playing of musical instruments, and who experiences waves of hair standing on end, trembling, and tears.

</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
The worship of the Deity <sub>śrī-vigraha-ārādhana</sub>, daily <sub>nitya</sub>, various <sub>nānā</sub>, clothing and ornaments <sub>śṛṅgāra</sub>, of His temple <sub>tat-mandira</sub>, in cleaning <sub>mārjana-ādau</sub>, of one who is engaged <sub>yuktasya</sub>, the devotees <sub>bhaktān</sub>, and <sub>ca</sub>, who engages <sub>niyuñjataḥ</sub>, also <sub>api</sub>, I offer obeisances <sub>vande</sub>, of my spiritual master <sub>guroḥ</sub>, unto the auspicious lotus feet <sub>śrī-caraṇa-aravindam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru, who is always engaged in the daily worship of the Deity with various decorations, cleaning His temple, and other such services, and who also engages the devotees in this worship.

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Of four kinds <sub>catur-vidha</sub>, sacred food remnants of the Supreme Lord <sub>śrī-bhagavat-prasāda</sub>, delicious foodstuffs <sub>svādv-anna</sub>, satisfied <sub>tṛptān</sub>, assemblies of the devotees of Lord Hari <sub>hari-bhakta-saṅghān</sub>, having indeed made <sub>kṛtvā-eva</sub>, satisfaction <sub>tṛptim</sub>, of the one who experiences <sub>bhajataḥ</sub>, always <sub>sadā-eva</sub>, I offer respectful obeisances <sub>vande</sub>, of the spiritual master <sub>guroḥ</sub>, unto the auspicious lotus feet <sub>śrī-caraṇa-aravindam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru, who always feels satisfaction only upon fully satisfying the assemblies of devotees of Lord Hari with the four kinds of delicious foodstuffs that are the prasāda of Lord Kṛṣṇa.

### Verse 5

<center>

</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Of Śrīmatī Rādhikā and Lord Mādhava <sub>śrī-rādhikā-mādhavayoḥ</sub>, of the unlimitedly sweet pastimes <sub>apāra-mādhurya-līlā-guṇa-rūpa-nāmnām</sub>, who is always eager for relishing at every moment <sub>prati-kṣaṇa-āsvādana-lolupasya</sub>, I offer obeisances <sub>vande</sub>, of the spiritual master <sub>guroḥ</sub>, unto the auspicious lotus feet <sub>śrī-caraṇa-aravindam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru, who is always eagerly hankering to relish at every moment the boundless sweetness of the pastimes, qualities, forms, and names of Śrī Rādhikā and Mādhava.

### Verse 6

</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Of the youthful couple of the groves <sub>nikuñja-yūnoḥ</sub>, for the perfection of amorous pastimes <sub>rati-keli-siddhyai</sub>, whichever <sub>yā</sub>, whatever by the female companions <sub>yā-ālibhiḥ</sub>, arrangement <sub>yuktiḥ</sub>, desirable <sub>apekṣaṇīyā</sub>, because of great expertise in that <sub>tatra-ati-dākṣyāt</sub>, of one who is very dear <sub>ati-vallabhasya</sub>, I offer obeisances <sub>vande</sub>, of the spiritual master <sub>guroḥ</sub>, unto the auspicious lotus feet <sub>śrī-caraṇa-aravindam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
The spiritual master is very dear, because he is expert in assisting the gopīs, who at different times make different tasteful arrangements for the perfection of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa’s conjugal loving affairs within the groves of Vṛndāvana. I offer my most respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of such a spiritual master.

### Verse 7

<center>

As directly the Supreme Lord Hari <sub>sākṣāt-dharitvena</sub>, by all the scriptures <sub>samasta-śāstraiḥ</sub>, declared <sub>uktaḥ</sub>, thus <sub>tathā</sub>, is meditated upon <sub>bhāvyate</sub>, indeed <sub>eva</sub>, by the saintly devotees <sub>sadbhiḥ</sub>, but <sub>kintu</sub>, of the Lord <sub>prabhoḥ</sub>, who <sub>yaḥ</sub>, dear <sub>priyaḥ</sub>, certainly <sub>eva</sub>, of him <sub>tasya</sub>, I offer obeisances <sub>vande</sub>, of the spiritual master <sub>guroḥ</sub>, unto the auspicious lotus feet <sub>śrī-caraṇa-aravindam</sub>.

**Translation:**  
The guru is proclaimed by all revealed scriptures to be directly Śrī Hari, and is accepted as such by saintly authorities, yet he is the most dear servitor of the Lord. I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of that Śrī Guru.

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**

**Translation:**  
By the mercy of the guru one attains the mercy of Lord Kṛṣṇa; without his mercy there is no shelter anywhere. Meditating upon and praising his glories three times a day, I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of Śrī Guru.

## 2. Vibhāvarī Śeṣa
**Author / Source:** *Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura (Kalyāṇa-kalpataru)*

</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Night <sub>vibhāvarī</sub>, is ending <sub>śeṣa</sub>, the entrance of light <sub>āloka-praveśa</sub>, sleep <sub>nidrā</sub>, giving up <sub>chāṛi'</sub>, arise <sub>uṭho</sub>, O living entity <sub>jīva</sub>, chant <sub>bolo</sub>, Hari <sub>hari</sub>, Hari <sub>hari</sub>, Mukunda <sub>mukunda</sub>, Murāri <sub>murāri</sub>, Rāma <sub>rāma</sub>, Kṛṣṇa <sub>kṛṣṇa</sub>, Hayagrīva <sub>hayagrīva</sub>.

**Translation:**  
The night has ended and the light of dawn is entering. Give up your sleep and arise, O jīva! Chant the holy names of Lord Hari: Mukunda, Murāri, Rāma, Kṛṣṇa, and Hayagrīva!

## 3. Namaste Narasiṁhāya
**Author / Source:** *Nṛsiṁha praṇāma (Caitanya-caritāmṛta 16.52–53; Gīta-govinda, Daśāvatāra-stotra 4)*

### Verse 1

<center>
namas te nara-siṁhāya<br/>
prahlādāhlāda-dāyine<br/>
hiraṇyakaśipor vakṣaḥ-<br/>
śilā-ṭaṅka-nakhālaye [1]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Obeisances <sub>namas</sub>, unto You <sub>te</sub>, unto Lord Narasiṁha <sub>nara-siṁhāya</sub>, unto the giver of joy to Prahlāda <sub>prahlāda-āhlāda-dāyine</sub>, of Hiraṇyakaśipu <sub>hiraṇyakaśipoḥ</sub>, unto Him whose nails are like chisels on the stone-like chest <sub>vakṣaḥ-śilā-ṭaṅka-nakha-ālaye</sub>.

**Translation:**  
I offer my obeisances to Lord Nṛsiṁha, who gives joy to Prahlāda Mahārāja and whose nails are like chisels on the stonelike chest of the demon Hiraṇyakaśipu.

---

### Verse 2

<center>
ito nṛsiṁhaḥ parato nṛsiṁho<br/>
yato yato yāmi tato nṛsiṁhaḥ<br/>
bahir nṛsiṁho hṛdaye nṛsiṁho<br/>

</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
On this side <sub>itaḥ</sub>, Lord Nṛsiṁha <sub>nṛsiṁhaḥ</sub>, on the other side <sub>parataḥ</sub>, Lord Nṛsiṁha <sub>nṛsiṁhaḥ</sub>, wherever <sub>yataḥ-yataḥ</sub>, I go <sub>yāmi</sub>, there <sub>tataḥ</sub>, Lord Nṛsiṁha <sub>nṛsiṁhaḥ</sub>, externally <sub>bahiḥ</sub>, Lord Nṛsiṁha <sub>nṛsiṁhaḥ</sub>, in the heart <sub>hṛdaye</sub>, Lord Nṛsiṁha <sub>nṛsiṁhaḥ</sub>, unto Lord Nṛsiṁha <sub>nṛsiṁham</sub>, the original source <sub>ādim</sub>, shelter <sub>śaraṇam</sub>, I surrender <sub>prapadye</sub>.

**Translation:**  
Lord Nṛsiṁha is here and He is also there. Wherever I go Lord Nṛsiṁha is there. He is in the heart and is outside as well. I surrender to Lord Nṛsiṁha, the origin of all things and the supreme refuge.

---

### Verse 3

<center>
tava kara-kamala-vare nakham adbhuta-śṛṅgaṁ<br/>
dalita-hiraṇyakaśipu-tanu-bhṛṅgam<br/>
keśava dhṛta-nara-hari-rūpa jaya jagadīśa hare<br/>
jaya jagadīśa hare jaya jagadīśa hare [3]
</center>

**Connected Word-for-Word Translation:**  
Your <sub>tava</sub>, upon the exquisite lotus hands <sub>kara-kamala-vare</sub>, the nails <sub>nakham</sub>, having wonderfully sharp tips <sub>adbhuta-śṛṅgam</sub>, which have torn apart the wasp-like body of Hiraṇyakaśipu <sub>dalita-hiraṇyakaśipu-tanu-bhṛṅgam</sub>, O Lord Keśava <sub>keśava</sub>, who have assumed the form of half-man <sub>dhṛta-nara-hari-rūpa</sub>, all glories <sub>jaya</sub>, O Lord of the universe <sub>jagad-īśa</sub>, O Lord Hari <sub>hare</sub>.

**Translation:**  
O Keśava! O Lord of the universe! O Lord Hari, who have assumed the form of half-man, half-lion! All glories to You! Just as one can easily crush a wasp between one’s fingernails, so in the same way the body of the wasp-like demon Hiraṇyakaśipu has been ripped apart by the wonderful pointed nails on Your beautiful lotus hands.

## 5. Ten Offenses to the Holy Name
**Author / Source:** *Padma Purāṇa (Brahma-khaṇḍa 25.15–18, 22–23; Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 11.521–526)*

### The Ten Offenses to the Holy Name (from Padma Purāṇa)

1. To blaspheme the devotees who have dedicated their lives for propagating the holy name of the Lord.
2. To consider the names of demigods like Lord Śiva or Lord Brahmā to be equal to, or independent of, the name of Lord Viṣṇu.
3. To disobey the orders of the spiritual master.
4. To blaspheme the Vedic literature or literature in pursuance of the Vedic version.
5. To consider the glories of chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa to be imagination.
6. To give some interpretation on the holy name of the Lord.
7. To commit sinful activities on the strength of the holy name of the Lord.
8. To consider chanting of Hare Kṛṣṇa one of the auspicious ritualistic activities offered in the Vedas as fruitive activities (karma-kāṇḍa).
9. To instruct a faithless person about the glories of the holy name.
10. To not have complete faith in the chanting of the holy names and to maintain material attachments, even after understanding so many instructions on this matter.

*It is also an offense to be inattentive while chanting.*  
*Every devotee who claims to be a Vaiṣṇava must guard against these offenses in order to quickly achieve the desired success — pure love for Lord Kṛṣṇa.*
